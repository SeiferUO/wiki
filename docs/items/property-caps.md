# PvM Property Caps

Every PvM property has a **global cap**: the most you can have from **all sources combined**, meaning your weapon, armor, clothing, jewelry, Shepherd's Crook and [PvM runes](ankhs-runes.md) in your Ankh. Anything above the cap is wasted.

- Roughly **65%** of a cap is meant to come from your **Ankh runes**, and **35%** from your **items**.
- Each item also has its own **per-item maximum** (see [below](#per-item-maximums)). For slots you wear several of (armor, clothing, jewelry), the per-item maximum is a share of the item part of the cap.
- See [Gear Properties](gear-properties.md) for what each property does.

## Per-item maximums

The highest value a single item can roll (from loot or crafting).

### Weapons (1 slot)

| Property | Max |
|---|---|
| Attack Speed, Accuracy, Mastery Damage, Boss Damage | 20 |
| Area Damage | 25 |
| Weapon Damage, Poison Resistance, Curse Resistance, Mage Killer | 15 |
| Life Leech, Critical Damage | 10 |

### Armor (6 slots)

Every armor property maxes at **5 per piece**: Hunter's Luck, Harvesting Luck, Crafting Luck, Archeologist, Reflective, Evasion, Poison and Curse Resistance, creature-type protections, Concoction Bonus, Warding Resonance and Scholarship.
