# Artifacts

Artifacts are named weapons and armor with **preset special properties**. Unlike normal crafted gear, they never roll random properties: each artifact always gets the same set, only the values are random. Artifact weapons also have a unique **artifact ability**.

## How artifact properties roll

- Each artifact rolls a value inside the range listed below for each of its properties.
- Artifacts that **drop** also get one **extra random property** (from the normal weapon or armor list, one they don't already have). Crafted artifacts only get it when they come out **Mastercrafted**.
- Dropped artifact weapons also roll random Accuracy, Damage and Durability levels (level 5 is the rarest, 10%). Dropped artifact armor rolls a Protection and Durability level and 75–125 durability.
- Crafted **Exceptional** artifact armor gets +1 on each preset property (never above the range's max).
- Artifacts can't be changed on the [Enchanting Table](enchanting-crafting.md#enchanting-table) or with Enhancement Scrolls.

## Where they come from

- **Artifact weapons** drop from Abominations, Hellfire minions, hunted creatures, sequence trap chests and the Shame quest. Blacksmiths can also craft them (110 skill, a recipe, gems and a Book of Lost Knowledge).
- **Artifact armor** comes from boss and [expedition](../quests/expeditions.md) artifact drops, and some can be crafted.

## Artifact abilities

Single-click an artifact weapon and choose **Overview** to see its ability. Abilities only work against **monsters** (not pets, summons or players).

- **Always on:** works on every hit. Stacking abilities build up over 3 hits and then restart; the stacks reset when you unequip the weapon.
- **On proc:** each hit has a chance of **Anatomy ÷ 400** (30% at 120 Anatomy), with a **15-second cooldown** shared by all artifact abilities.
