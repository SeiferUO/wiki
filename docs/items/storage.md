# Storage Items

Storage items hold large amounts of one kind of item without taking up house storage for each piece. Most of them work the same way:

- Double-click to open the storage window.
- **Add:** target an item, a **container** (adds everything valid inside it) or **your backpack** (adds everything valid you carry). Items must be in your backpack.
- **Withdraw:** click the item in the window. It goes to your backpack (or at your feet if your backpack is full).

## Storage Shelf

The **Storage Shelf** stores everyday supplies by type and amount. It must be **locked down in a house**, and you need access to it.

- **Categories:** Consumables (reagents, potions, bandages and more), Gems, Instruments, Weapons, Armor and Other. Over 160 item types.
- **Adding:** target items, a container, yourself (your whole backpack) or your **pet** (its whole pack). You can also add from your bank box.
- **Withdrawing:** pick an amount, up to **10,000** at a time.
- **Restock Shelf:** puts supplies you're carrying back on the shelf.

### Character Loadouts

The shelf can save up to **10 loadout profiles**: lists of items and amounts you want to carry.

- Add items from the shelf to the selected profile, then click **Resupply** to pull exactly what's missing from your backpack.
- **Bag Loadout:** resupplied items go into a **Loadout Bag** instead of loose in your backpack.
- **Equip:** weapons and armor in the loadout are equipped directly when possible.

## Magic Item Vault

The **Magic Item Vault** stores magic gear. It must be **locked down or secured in a house**, and you need access to it.

- **Categories:** weapons, armor, spellbooks, Shepherd's Crooks, mage staves and jewelry, up to **250 of each**.
- Blessed and insured items can't be stored.
- Search by **quality** and **magic property**, and sort by name or **intensity**. Each item shows its intensity score.
- Use **Store Item** and target an item, or a container to store everything valid inside it.

## Tailor's Wardrobe

The **Tailor's Wardrobe** (armoire) stores clothing. It must be **locked down or secured in a house**. Target a piece of clothing, a bag of clothing, or yourself to store everything you carry. See also the [Wardrobe](wardrobe.md).

## Dye tomes and the Dyes Storage Shelf

Dye tomes keep your collected dyes and hues in one place: **Armament**, **Backpack**, **Beard**, **Hair**, **Skin**, **Carpet**, **Furniture**, **Jewelry**, **Runebook**, **Spellbook**, **Rare Cloth** and **Tincture Vial** tomes.

The **Dyes Storage Shelf** holds all of those tomes together, and can fill them from the dyes in your backpack and its containers.
