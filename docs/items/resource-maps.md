# Resource Maps

A **Resource Map** leads to a special **harvesting node**: a giant ore vein or a giant tree that showers you with high-grade resources.

There are two kinds:

| Map | Node | Resource per hit |
|---|---|---|
| **Mining** resource map | a copper, dull copper, shadow iron, bronze, golden, agapite, verite or valorite mining node | 2–5 ore |
| **Lumberjacking** resource map | a giant oak, ash, yew, heartwood, bloodwood or frostwood tree | 10–25 logs |

The map's **color** tells you which resource its node gives.

## Using a map

1. Double-click the map in your backpack and confirm. The node appears at a random spot in the world, and its location is written on the map.
2. Double-click the map again any time to see the coordinates.
3. The node lasts **2 hours**. Its tooltip shows the minutes left.

## Harvesting the node

**Attack the node** with your weapon. Every hit:

- takes 5% of its health,
- puts **ore or logs straight into your backpack**,
- gives **Artisan** mastery XP and counts toward resource-gathering [weekly tasks](../quests/daily-weekly.md#weekly-tasks).

A node holds **100 charges**. Every time it loses another 10% of its health, **guardians** may appear around you (up to 10 per node), so bring friends or be ready to fight.

## Where to find maps

Resource maps come from magic loot drops, [rare loot](rare-loot.md), [dungeon chests](treasure.md#dungeon-chests), boss tokens and other rewards. Store them in a **Resource Maps Tome** (see [Storage Items](storage.md)).
