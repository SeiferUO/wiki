# Commodity Deeds

A **commodity deed** turns a big stack of resources into a single, light deed. It's great for storage and trading.

## Getting one

Buy an empty commodity deed from any **banker** or **provisioner** for **5 gold**.

## Filling a deed

1. Put the **empty deed** and the **resource stack** in your **bank box**, or both in the same **secure container** in a house.
2. Double-click the deed and target the stack.
3. The deed stores up to its limit for that resource (below). Anything over the limit stays in the stack.

The deed turns into *a filled commodity order*, takes the look and color of what's inside, and shows **Contains: amount name**. One deed holds **one resource type**.

## Redeeming

Double-click a filled deed while it's in your **bank box** or a **secure container**. The full amount drops into that container and the deed is used up.

## What can be stored

| Resource | Max per deed |
|---|---|
| **Ingots**: iron, dull copper, shadow iron, copper, bronze, gold, agapite, verite, valorite, azurite | 5,000 |
| **Leather**: regular, spined, horned, barbed, scaled | 5,000 |
| **Hides**: regular, spined, horned, barbed, scaled | 5,000 |
| **Boards**: regular, oak, ash, yew, heartwood, bloodwood, frostwood | 5,000 |
| **Reagents**: black pearl, bloodmoss, garlic, ginseng, mandrake root, nightshade, sulfurous ash, spider's silk | 5,000 |
| Arrows, bolts | 5,000 |
| Bandages | 10,000 |
| Feathers, blank scrolls | 2,500 |
| Empty bottles | 500 |
| **Necromancy reagents**: bat wing, daemon blood, nox crystal, pig iron, grave dust | 500 |
| **Imbuing ingredients**: magical residue, enchanted essence, relic fragment, chaga mushroom, spider carapace, bottle of ichor, reflective wolf eye, faery dust, fey wings, boura pelt, silver snake skin, void orb, vial of vitriol, slith tongue, raptor teeth, daemon claw, goblin blood, lava serpent crust, undying flesh, crystalline blackrock, delicate scales, arcanic rune stone, powdered iron, crushed glass, elven fletching, crystal shards, lodestone, seed of renewal, parasitic plant, luminescent fungi | 500 |
| **Gems**: fire ruby, white pearl, blue diamond, turquoise; lucky coin | 500 |

Anything not on this list can't be stored ("That type of item cannot be stored in this deed").

## Trading

Filled deeds can be used on the **Global Vendor** to fill buy orders. The deed's resource and quantity must match the order **exactly**. Commodity deeds are worth nothing at [Griphook](griphook.md).
