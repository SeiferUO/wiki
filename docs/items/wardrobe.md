# Wardrobe

The **Wardrobe** saves clothing appearances so you can show them over whatever you're actually wearing (transmog). Open it with **[wardrobe** or from the Compendium hotbar.

## Adding styles

- **Clothing:** use **Add to Wardrobe** on a piece in your backpack and confirm. **The item is destroyed** and its look (item, hue and layer) is saved.
- **Wardrobe Style Deeds:** double-click to unlock a style without owning the item. Some are sold in the Loyalty Store.
- You can't add the same look twice, and you can't add temporary seasonal reward shrouds, tabards or sashes, or cursed (MOBA) items.

Styles work on these layers: cloak, head, arms, gloves, neck, waist, shirt, middle torso, robe, pants, inner legs, outer legs and shoes. Jewelry is not included.

## Using styles

Pick a saved style for a layer and it shows over the item you're wearing there. Clear a layer to show your real gear again.
You can search and filter by layer. Deleting a saved style removes it for good.

## Hues

Each layer can show the style's own hue (**Entry Hue**) or a **Custom Hue** from the **Hue Shop**:

| Hue | Cost to unlock |
|---|---|
| Normal hues | 50 Platinum Coins |
| Pure black and flashy/rare hues | 150 Platinum Coins |

Instead of coins you can pay with **25 special cloth** of that hue. An unlocked hue works on **all** layers.
