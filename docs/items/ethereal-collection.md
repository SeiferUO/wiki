# Ethereal Collection

The **Ethereal Collection** is an ethereal statuette that can take the look of any style you've unlocked. Collect looks once, then switch your mount's appearance whenever you like (transmog for mounts).

## Opening it

Single-click the Ethereal Collection in your backpack and choose **Overview**. The menu shows your **Platinum Coins**, how many styles you've unlocked, and every style.

- **All / Unlocked / Locked** filter the list.
- Click a style to see it in the **Style Details** panel: its name, status and whether it can be dyed.

## Unlocking styles

Every collection starts with the **Horse** look for free. There are two ways to unlock more:

- **Platinum Coins:** select a locked style and press **Unlock**. The coins must be in your backpack.
- **Trade in a statuette:** press **Add Statuette** and target an ethereal statuette in your backpack. **The statuette is destroyed** and its style is unlocked.
    - Ethereals **with charges** can't be traded in.
    - The statuette can't be ridden at the time, and its look must be one of the collection's styles.
    - If you already have that style, the statuette is refused, so nothing is lost.
    - Hues collected on the statuette are turned back into **ethereal dyes** and sent to your **bank**.

Unlocked styles belong to **that character** and stay unlocked for good.

## Changing the look

Select an unlocked style and press **Select Style**. You must be dismounted. The collection keeps its look until you change it again.

## Dyes

**Ethereal dyes** and the **Retouching Tool** (switches between the see-through ethereal look and the normal look) work on the collection like on any ethereal. A few styles **can't be dyed**: they're marked **\*Undyable** in the menu and with a **\*** below. While wearing one, the collection can't be dyed either.
