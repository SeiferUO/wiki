# Gear Properties

Weapons, armor, clothing, jewelry, spellbooks, mage staves and Shepherd's Crooks can carry **special properties**. They come from loot, [crafting](enchanting-crafting.md#crafting) and [enchanting](enchanting-crafting.md#enchanting-table).

!!! note "How the limits work"
    - **Item max** is the most one item can have of that property.
    - **Global cap** is the most you can have **in total** from all sources: every equipped item plus your [PvM runes](ankhs-runes.md).
    - Most properties only work in **PvM**.

## How many properties an item can have

| Item | Max special properties |
|---|---|
| Weapon, armor, spellbook, mage staff, crook | **3** (crafted items that came out with 4 can keep 4) |
| Clothing, jewelry | **1** (when enchanting) |

## Weapons

| Property | What it does | Item max | Global cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attack Speed | Faster swings | 20 | 50 |
| Accuracy | Higher hit chance | 20 | 50 |
| Weapon Damage | More weapon damage | 15 | 75 |
| Boss Damage | More damage to bosses | 20 | 50 |
| Critical Hit Chance | Chance for a critical hit | 10 | 30 |
| Area Damage | Chance to hit every monster around the target (Felucca only) | 25 | — |
| Life Leech | Chance to heal for part of the damage dealt | 10 | 30 |
| Mastery Damage | More mastery ability damage | 20 | 50 |
| Mage Killer | More damage to spellcasting monsters | 15 | 50 |
| Savagery | Chance to trigger [Savagery](../core/weapons.md) | 5 | 10 |
| Poison Resistance | Resist poison | 15 | 75 |
| Curse Resistance | Resist curses | 15 | 75 |

## Armor

Each armor piece can carry up to **5** of a property. With 6 armor slots, a full suit fills most of a property's global cap.

| Property | What it does | Item max | Global cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poison / Curse Resistance | Resist poison / curses | 5 | 75 |
| Vermin, Undead, Arachnid, Daemonic, Draconic, Elemental, Giant, Infidel Protection | Less damage from that creature type | 5 each | 25 each |
| Reflective | Chance to send a melee hit's damage back to the monster | 5 | 30 |
| Evasion | Chance to avoid monster attacks | 5 | 30 |
| Hunter's Luck | Better loot | 5 | 50 |
| Harvesting Luck | Chance for a double harvest | 5 | 50 |
| Crafting Luck | Better crafting results | 5 | 100 |
| Scholarship | +1% XP per point | 5 | 50 |
| Archeologist | While harvesting, a 0.5% chance per point to dig up an archeology find (45 s cooldown, 60 s in dungeons) | 5 | 10 |
| Concoction Bonus | Stronger potions (heal amount, cure chance, buff duration, explosion damage) | 5 | 50 |
| Warding Resonance | Longer buff durations | 5 | 50 |

Loot armor can also roll **Focus**, **Magic Reflection**, **Gluttony**, **Savagery**, **Spell Damage**, **Lower Reagent Cost** and **Lower Mana Cost**.

## Clothing

Clothing uses the same armor properties as above. It can also carry these:

| Property | What it does | Item max | Global cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Attack Speed, Accuracy, Weapon Damage, Mastery Damage, Critical Damage, Savagery | as on weapons | as on weapons | as on weapons |
| Lower Reagent / Mana Cost, Surging, Spell Leech | as on spellbooks | as on spellbooks | as on spellbooks |
| Concoction Bonus | Stronger potions | 15 | 50 |
| Reactive Paralyze | Chance to paralyze a monster that hits you, for 2.5 s | 15 | 25 |
| Focus | Chance to keep casting when hit | 15 | 50 |
| Spell Damage | More spell damage | 15 | 50 |

## Spellbooks and mage staves

| Property | What it does | Item max | Global cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lower Reagent Cost | Chance to cast without reagents | 30 | 100 |
| Lower Mana Cost | Spells cost less mana | 20 | 40 |
| Surging | Chance for a spell damage surge | 20 | 50 |
| Spell Leech | Chance to heal for the spell's mana cost | 10 | 30 |
| Casting Speed | Faster casting | 15 | 50 |
| Casting Recovery | Shorter recovery between spells | 15 | 25 |
| Mastery Damage | More mastery ability damage | 10 | 50 |
| Summon Heal | Your summons heal for part of the damage they deal | 20 | 50 |
| Summon Surge | Chance for your summons to surge | 5 | 25 |
| Magic Arrow, Harm, Fireball, Lightning, Mind Blast, Energy Bolt, Explosion, Flamestrike | +% damage with that spell | 50 each | — |
| Summon Creature, Blade Spirits, Earth / Air / Water / Fire Elemental, Energy Vortex, Summon Daemon | +% stats for that summon (max 50%) | 35 each | — |

A spellbook or staff can only roll **one** summon-spell property.
