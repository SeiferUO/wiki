# Ankhs & PvM Runes

Two separate systems:

- **Mastery Ankhs** (PvM runes) are socketed into character-wide **slots** and give PvM bonuses.
- **Ankh Necklaces** are an earring-slot item with one fixed bonus.

## Mastery Ankhs (PvM runes)

### Slots

You don't socket ankhs into gear. They go into **ankh slots on your character**. You unlock slots with **Rune XP**, which you earn from killing monsters.

- Up to **30 slots**.
- Each slot costs more XP than the last: about 54,000 XP for slot 1, 204,000 for slot 10, 622,000 for slot 20 and 1.3 million for slot 30.
- Removing an ankh returns it to your backpack unchanged.

### Tiers

| Tier | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Bronze | monsters, chests, quests, paragon chests |
| Silver | monsters, chests, quests, bosses |
| Gold | chests, bosses, Peerless, Doom, level 6–7 treasure maps |
| **Platinum** | Champion and Harrower Void Skulls only |

Drops are weighted toward lower tiers (Bronze 8 : Silver 5 : Gold 3 : Platinum 1).

### Stacking limit

Ankhs of the same property **add together**, up to a **per-character maximum** (the "Max" column below).
That total is then still limited by the item property caps on [Weapons](../core/weapons.md) and [Spellcasting](../core/spellcasting.md).

**Most bonuses only work in PvM.**

## Ankh Necklaces

Worn in the **earring** slot. Each necklace has one bonus:

| Necklace | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Gold | +10% gold from creatures |
| Harvesting | 5% chance of double harvest yields |
| Silver | +10% damage to undead |
| Daemon Slayer | +15% damage to daemons |
| Cleric | **+15%** healing from spells and bandages |
| Friendship | **+10%** weapon and spell damage when a party or guild member (not on your IP) is within 12 tiles |
| Alchemist | 25% chance not to use up a potion |
| Savagery | +5% chance to trigger Savagery |
| Reactive Armor | 5% chance to deal a monster's hit back to it and take no damage |
| Poison Resistance | Full resistance to creature poison |
| Gluttony | +25% hunger satiation |
