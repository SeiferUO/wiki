# Griphook

**Griphook** is a goblin collector. He buys rare items for **Achievement Points (AP)** and runs three 5-for-1 trade-ins. Stand within 12 tiles of him to trade.

## Hanging around Griphook

- **Every hour**, every player within 50 tiles gets **+1 AP**. If 5 or more players are there, one random player gets **+5 AP** more.
- Hidden players within 25 tiles are revealed every minute.
- Every hour he also stables pets near him and pets of players who have been offline for 48+ hours.

## Selling items for AP

Use **Appraise** to see what an item is worth. You can turn items in one at a time or as a whole container. One transaction is capped at **1,000 AP** *(default)*, so split big stacks.

| Item | AP |
|---|---|
| Talisman relic | 500 each |
| Unchained pet relic | 50 |
| Book of Lost Knowledge, Skill Cap Ball | 25 each |
| Rose (Magical, or a city rose with 25+ charges) | 15 |
| Legendary / Enhanced / Standard pet power scroll | 10 / 7 / 5 |
| Mastery orb | 5 each |
| Hued light source (looted, rare hue) | 5 |
| Magical bracelet, earrings or chain | 5 |
| Unfinished treasure map | its level |
| Runic tool | its uses left |
| Spell scroll, spell hue deed, dye | 2 each |
| Unrevealed mining, lumberjacking or waterstained map | 2 |
| Orb fragment, Book of Lost Knowledge page, runic component, arcane wool | 2 each |
| Voting token, Compassion tincture, Expedition ticket, Turret Defense token, Golden ticket | 2 each |
| Dungeon cloth | 1 per piece |
| Decorations, rare footwear, decorative flooring, paragon chests | 1 |

Some items are worth nothing to him: power scrolls (use the trade-in instead), bank checks, commodity deeds, repair deeds and vendor contracts.

## Trade-ins (5 for 1)

Each trade-in collects 5 items, then you **claim** the reward. It goes to your bank box.

| Trade in 5 | Get |
|---|---|
| **Power scrolls** | A random **+1 power scroll** for one of 30 skills: crafting, harvesting, bard, taming, thief and utility skills |
| **Mastery Ankhs** (PvM runes) | A random Mastery Ankh. Its tier is the **average** tier of the 5 you gave, rounded, but never above **Gold**. |
| **Mastery orbs** | An orb of the mastery you pick in **Orb Preference** |
