# Harvesting Gear

**Helga the Journeywoman** sells **Journeyman kits** for Mining, Lumberjacking and Fishing. Each kit piece levels up as you harvest.

## Buying

Each piece costs **100,000 gold** (from your bank) and **2,500 Artificer Points**. You also need a number of **orders completed for Helga** this season:

| Piece | Orders needed |
|---|---|
| Hat / Cap | 20 |
| Boots | 25 |
| Gloves | 25 (Fishing) / 35 (Lumberjacking, Mining) |
| Vest / Apron | 30 |
| Belt | 40 |

You earn Artificer Points from bulk orders, weekly tasks and global quests. The pieces are blessed.

## What each piece does

Each piece gives a bonus **per level** (max level **5**) to its own harvesting skill:

| Piece | Per level | At level 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Hat | +1 skill | +5 Mining / Lumberjacking / Fishing |
| Vest | +1% harvest yield | +5% |
| Boots | −0.1 s harvest delay | −0.5 s |
| Gloves | +5% chance your tool doesn't lose durability | 25% |
| Belt | +2% **Prospector** chance | 10% |

**Prospector:**

- **Mining:** the ore can turn into a richer ore.
- **Lumberjacking:** the log can turn into a rarer wood.
- **Fishing:** you get an extra catch.

## Leveling

Wear the pieces while you harvest with **their** skill. Every successful harvest gives XP based on what you harvested:

| Resource | XP |
|---|---|
| Iron ore / plain logs | 5 |
| Dull Copper / Oak | 6 |
| Shadow Iron / Ash | 7 |
| Copper | 8 |
| Bronze / Yew | 9 |
| Gold | 10 |
| Heartwood | 11 |
| Agapite | 12 |
| Verite / Bloodwood | 14 |
| Valorite / Frostwood | 17 |
| Fish / big fish | 6 / 12 |

The XP is **split** between all the matching pieces you're wearing that aren't maxed yet: each gets XP / (pieces × 1.15).

| Level | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Total XP | 15,000 | 50,000 | 115,000 | 220,000 | 370,000 |
