# Invasions

An invasion is a server-wide event: an army of themed monsters attacks a region in **10 waves**, ending with a boss. Everyone who helps earns rewards.

## How an invasion starts

The server builds up **seasonal invasion points**. When they reach **100**, the season's invasion begins in its target region *(theme and region set by staff)*.

| Source | Seasonal points |
|---|---|
| Completing a daily quest | 0.5 (easy) / 1 (medium) / 2 (hard) |
| Completing a weekly task | 1 |
| Completing a global quest | 2 |
| Killing a Legendary Loot Goblin | 1 |
| Seasonal event items (Halloween, Easter, event vendor) | 1 |
| Monster kills during some seasonal events (Crimson Courtship) | 0.02–1 by monster strength |

Points don't build up while an invasion is running. The invasion controller shows the current progress.

When it starts, there's a server broadcast and a Discord message, and a **gate** opens in Britain. Murderers can't use the gate.

## Waves

- The invasion spawns its army over **9 waves**, getting harder as it goes:

| Waves | Easy | Medium | Hard monsters |
|---|---|---|---|
| First third | 70% | 30% | — |
| Middle third | 25% | 50% | 25% |
| Last third | 20% | 30% | 50% |

- The next wave comes once only a handful of monsters are left (checked every 30 seconds).
- **Wave 10 is the boss.** The invasion is won once the boss falls with 10 or fewer monsters left. Use **[Invasion** to see the leaderboard.
- In guarded towns, invasion monsters are immune to guards.

## Invasion score

You score for every invasion monster that dies while you have looting rights on it and are within **30 tiles**. Your pets' damage counts.

| Monster | Score |
|---|---|
| Mini-boss or stronger | 50 (×3 after the boss has appeared) |
| Abomination | 30 |
| Legendary | 25 |
| Elite | 20 |
| Hard | 15 |
| Medium | 10 |
| Easy | 5 |
| Weaker | 1–2 |

Everyone who scores shares the credit, so a group effort pays everyone.

## Rewards

When the invasion is defeated, every participant gets *(dividers set by staff)*:

- **Event points**, **Monthly Challenge points** and **Achievement Points**, all based on your score.
- A **special-hued item** in your bank if your score is at least **25% of the median** participant's score.

The top 3 are announced to the server.

### Invasion loot

Each theme has a **limited stock** of special items for the whole invasion. When an invasion monster dies, the **top damager** may receive one:

- Rare, medium and common items each have their own chance *(set by staff)*.
- Chances are low in early waves (10% of the normal chance at wave 1) and reach full strength by the last wave.
- Once an item's stock runs out, it can't drop again that invasion.

## Invasion zones

Inside an **invasion zone** you don't lose items when you die. Recall, Gate Travel, Sacred Journey and Mark don't work there.
