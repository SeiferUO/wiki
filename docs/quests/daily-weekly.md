# Daily & Weekly Quests

## Daily quests

Talk to the **Central Questmaster** to see every daily quest. Each quest can be done **once every 24 hours**.

### Rewards

Every completed daily gives:

- **One random reward** from the table for its difficulty (sent to your **bank box**),
- **Reward points:** Easy 3–5, Medium 4–6, Hard 5–8,
- **XP** for all your progression systems (25 × reward points),
- monthly challenge points (150 × reward points),
- a **5% chance** for a Bronze or Silver [Mastery Ankh](../items/ankhs-runes.md),
- a chance for an Unchained raffle token when a raffle is running (7.5% Easy, 15% Medium, 25% Hard),
- seasonal invasion points,
- 10 minutes off the next [Global Quest](global.md) cooldown.

**Every 5 dailies** you also get a **mastery orb** for your active mastery.

## Weekly tasks

Visit the **Quest Master** (the Weekly Task Giver) to pick up tasks. Progress is tracked with your **Weekly Quest Talisman**.

- You get **15 tasks per week** per account. Everything resets weekly.
- You can only have one active task of each type, creature or dungeon. Each task can be finished once per week.
- Completing a task has a **5% chance** that Lord British gives you **an extra task**. Vice vs Virtue region buffs add more.
- More tasks can be bought with Arcane Dust or a **Letter of Recommendation** (from dungeon event quests).
- You can't abandon a finished task. Resetting your talisman has a 1-hour cooldown.

### Task types

| Task | Goal | Reward points |
|---|---|---|
| Monster Hunt | Kill a number of one creature type | Achievement points scaled by the creature's tier |
| Paragon Hunt | Kill paragon creatures | 2 per kill |
| Legendary Hunt | Kill Legendary-tier creatures | 0.35 per kill |
| Boss Hunt | Kill bosses | 10 per kill |
| Mini-Boss Hunt | Kill mini-bosses | 5 per kill |
| Special Hunt | Kill a category of creatures (undead, daemons, etc.) | scaled |
| Dungeon Hunt | Kill creatures in a specific dungeon | scaled |
| Treasure Hunt | Unlock treasure chests | 2 per chest |

In **Special Hunt** and **Dungeon Hunt** tasks, harder creatures count for more: Creep, Easy and Medium creatures count **1**, Hard and Elite count **2**, and Legendary or tougher count **3**.
| Resource Gathering | Mine, chop or gather resources | Artificer points |
| Crafting | Craft items from a given material | Artificer points |

Combat tasks give **Achievement Points** and **Monthly Challenge Points**. Gathering and crafting tasks give Achievement Points and **Artificer Points**.

### Citizen rewards

Each finished task can also earn a gift from a town citizen:

| Task | Chance of a gift |
|---|---|
| Legendary Hunt | 100% |
| Paragon Hunt | 95% |
| Crafting | 40% |
| Other tasks | 35% |

Gifts include bags of gold, XP tokens, special food, mastery orbs, ribbons, trophies, roses and rare-hued items.
