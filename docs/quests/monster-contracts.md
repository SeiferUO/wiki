# Monster Contracts

Hunting monsters can turn up a **Bounty Hunter Contract**: a job to slay more of the same creature. Finish it within **3 days** and the reward goes straight to your bank.

## Getting a contract

- Any monster you kill has a **1% chance** to drop a contract for **its own kind**. It lands in the corpse.
- Only monsters of **Medium** difficulty and up give contracts, up to **Legendary**.
- If several players earned the kill, the contract drops for **one** of them.

!!! info "Monsters that never drop contracts"
    Sea creatures, human-shaped monsters, champion spawn minions, expedition minions, chest guardians, animated dead, summoned or tamed creatures, and evolved monthly-challenge bosses. Kills in the Britain Zoo don't count.

## Working on it

- Keep the contract in your **backpack**. Every kill of that exact creature adds one to it, e.g. `Monster Contract (a wyvern): 12/40`.
- You need **looting rights** on the kill (you helped kill it), and you must be within **30 tiles** and not inside a house.
- Group hunting works: **everyone** with looting rights and a matching contract gets the kill.
- Several contracts for the same creature all progress at once.
- **Double-click** the contract to see your progress and the rewards waiting for you.

### How many kills?

| Difficulty | Kills needed |
|---|---|
| Medium | 45 to 75 |
| Hard, Elite, Legendary | 15 to 50 |

The contract's tooltip shows its difficulty, the creature and your progress.

!!! warning "3-day limit"
    A contract crumbles **3 days** after it drops, finished or not.

## Rewards

When the last kill lands, you hear a chime and everything goes **to your bank box**:

| Reward | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Gold** | A bank check. Tougher monsters pay more per kill, **up to 10,000 gold** |
| **Achievement Points (AP)** | Legendary: 1 per kill · Elite: 1 per 2 kills · Medium & Hard: 1 per 4 kills (**max 100**). They also count toward the monthly [World Ranking](../core/leaderboards.md) |
| **Mastery experience** | Medium: 100–200 · Hard: 250–450 · Elite: 500–700 · Legendary: 800–1,000, given to all your [experience](../core/experience.md) systems |
| **Statuette** | A random monster statuette: **10%** chance on Elite and Legendary contracts, **5%** on the rest |

The contract window shows the exact gold and points for your contract before you finish it.

## Tips

- **Short contracts on tough monsters** (15 kills of an Elite) are worth far more than long ones on easy creatures.
- Hunting with friends speeds things up: one kill counts for everyone in the fight who has the same contract.
- Contracts are **blessed**, so you keep them when you die.
