# Global Events

Each major dungeon (and a few outdoor areas) runs its own **event**: a short storyline with objectives that earn **event points**. Every event keeps its own points, and once a week you can trade them for rewards.

Open the **Global Events** window to see every event. Pick an event on the left to see its story, its three objectives, its hue and title, its reward tiers and your points. At the top left, a timer shows when claims reset.

## How it works

1. **Earn points** by completing the event's objectives (see each event below).
2. **Claim** from the Global Events window with the button at the bottom of the event's page. You're asked to confirm first.
3. You can claim **once per event per week**. Claims reset every **Monday at 04:55 (server time)**. An event's name is **red** in the list when you've already claimed it this week.
4. You need at least **50 points** to claim.

!!! info "What a claim gives and costs"
    A claim gives you **every tier you've reached**, but only takes the points of your **highest** tier. With 620 points in a 750-tier event, you get the 500, 250, 100 and 50 rewards for 500 points, and keep 120 for next week. Points you don't spend **carry over**.

## Reward tiers

Most events use these tiers:

| Points | Reward |
|---|---|
| **50** | 100 reward points |
| **100** | The event's **title** |
| **250** | A random item in the event's **hue** |
| **500** | A random **decoration** from the event's set |
| **750** | One random **rare** from the event's list |

The exceptions are:

- **Bilgewater** and **Luna**: the top tier is **1,000** points and there is no 750 tier.
- **Destard (Bonfire)**: the decoration tier is **400** points instead of 500.

Items go to your **bank box**. The rares of each event are listed below.

## The events

### The Secrets of Belial (Deceit)

Title: **The Netherbound**

| Objective | Points |
|---|---|
| Buy a **rune book** from Paul at the dungeon entrance, then add **Eldritch Runes** found on slain creatures | 5 per rune added |
| Slay a **Primeval Lich** | 5 for the top damage dealer, 3 for other players who helped |
| Slay a **dignitary skeleton** | 10 for the top damage dealer, 5 for other players who helped |
| Bring a dignitary's **remains** to Paul | 15 |
| Escort a **lost soul** out of the dungeon | 10 |

**750 rares:** Ethereal Skeletal Steed · Belial Arcane Robe · Lich, Skeleton or Ghoul mask · Doom Master Key · 2 dungeon cloth · skin dye · ethereal dye · a rare-hued item

## Seasonal events

Staff turn these on for their season, and they then appear in the same window:

| Event | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Easter Hunt** | Giant rabbits, capybaras and nature spirits. Tiers 50 / 100 / 250 / 400 / 600, with a 5% chance of an Ethereal Capybara at 600. Title: **The Bunny Basher** |
| **Crimson Courtship** (Valentine's) | Hunt the Crimson Court creatures and bring their items to the event NPC; rarer items are worth more. Tiers 50 / 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000, with a small chance of an Ethereal Unicorn. Title: **The Lover** |
| **Halloween** | Collect body parts (torso, brain, heart, limbs) and skull decorations |
| **Holiday / Krampus** | Decorate the Great Tree in Britain with ornaments, gather snowman parts and Glacia cores in Glacia, and beware Krampus |

- **Easter and Crimson Courtship** claims use **all** your points, not only the highest tier, and their rewards go to your **backpack**.
- **Halloween and Holiday** show your score, rank and a leaderboard in the window.
