# Mysterious Creatures

A collection quest for tamers. **15 rare creatures** hide across the realm, one in each of 15 regions. Find them all, tame them and bring them to **Cigal** in Britain to earn a **Mysterious Egg**.

!!! tip "Finding them is part of the quest"
    The regions aren't listed here: discovering where each creature lives is up to you. Once you've brought a creature to Cigal, your collection shows where you found it.

## How a creature appears

- Each creature belongs to one region. When **enough monsters have been killed** in that region, its creature reveals itself near the last kill.
- It **wanders for a while**, then vanishes if nobody tames it. After that it needs more kills (and some time) before it can appear again.
- Only kills by players (and their pets and summons) count.

## Taming and delivering

- Every mysterious creature needs **110 Animal Taming**.
- They're gentle: they **can't be hurt** and **can't fight**. They can't be stabled or shrunk either.
- Lead your tamed creature to **Cigal** and stay close by. He takes it as soon as it's next to him, and it counts as **found** in your collection.
- Bringing a kind you've already found is welcome too, but it doesn't add to your collection.

## Your collection

- Type **[mysterious** (or **[mc**) to see your collection: all 15 creatures, the ones you've found and where you found them. Unfound creatures show **???**.
- Cigal's window shows it too.

## The reward

- When you've found **all 15**, a **Mysterious Egg** is sent to your **bank box**, and the realm hears about it.
- Your collection then **starts over**: find them all again for another egg.
- **Hatching the egg** needs **50 Animal Taming** and **50 Animal Lore**, the egg in your backpack and room for a new follower. It hatches into a random [Unchained pet](../pets/unchained-list.md), already **bonded** to you.
