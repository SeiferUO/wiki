# Achievements & Titles

Type **[achievements** to open your **Achievement Tracker** (you get one automatically). It must stay in your backpack to record progress.

## How it works

- Each achievement has **4 tiers**. Every tier gives:
    - a **title** you can display,
    - the achievement's **artifact clothing** piece at that tier (sent to your bank box). A higher tier gives a stronger version.
    - **reward points**: 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000.
- **Kill progress** goes to everyone with looting rights within 75 tiles, and only while you [can earn XP](../core/experience.md#when-you-cant-earn-xp).
    - Each kill is worth **1 + creature HP / 5** (max 1,500; half for champion spawn creatures).
    - Slayer achievements get half of that.
- Boss achievements count **kills** (+1 each), not XP.
