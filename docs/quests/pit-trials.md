# Pit Trials (Gauntlet)

The Gauntlet is a **30-minute wave survival** challenge. Clear as many waves as you can, alone or with your party. Everything you earn is shared at the end, and your run goes on the leaderboard.

## Entering

- Use a **Pit Trial Stone** and choose **Start Gauntlet Trial**.
- Entry costs **5 Realm Defender (Turret Defense) Tokens**, unless the stone is set to free entry.
- Each stone has a few arenas. If they're all in use, wait for one to free up.
- **Groups:** invite nearby players from the stone. Everyone must be in **your party**.

## The run

- Waves start after a **30-second** countdown. Then you have **30 minutes**.
- Clear a wave and the next one comes **5 seconds** later. There are up to **100 waves**.
- Waves get tougher as you go:

| Waves | Monster tier |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | Trivial |
| 6–15 | Easy |
| 16–25 | Normal |
| 26–40 | Hard |
| 41–55 | Veteran |
| 56–70 | Legendary |
| 71–75 | Boss |
| 76–100 | Abomination |

  Wave size changes too: 50 creatures at the start, down to 15 around wave 45, back up to 40 at the end. Creatures also get a little more HP and aggro range each wave.

- **Dying:** you can't bring supplies in mid-run. The only way to restock is to die, resurrect and use **Re-enter Arena**, which costs you time.
- **Rules inside:** no PvP (players and pets can't hurt each other), you can only loot your own corpse, you don't lose items, and no Recall, Gate Travel, Sacred Journey or Mark.
- Pit Trial monsters give no guild prestige and no [rare loot](../items/rare-loot.md) kill points.

## Rewards

Every kill adds **gold** to the team pot. Gold per kill grows with the wave, from 10 at wave 1 up to thousands in the late waves. When the run ends (time up or wave 100):

- **Gold:** the pot is split evenly, **up to 150,000 per player**, as a bank check.
- **Your score** is your share of the gold. It gives **Monthly Challenge points** (score ÷ 50, max 2,500) and **Achievement Points** (score ÷ 100, max 250).
- **Spoils** go into a shared pool and are handed out **round-robin** to the team's bank boxes:

| Spoil | How many / chance |
|---|---|
| Special items | 1 per 10 waves (at least 1) |
| Treasure maps | 1 per 10 waves (at least 1), +10% chance of one more. Level 1–3 below wave 20, 2–4 below wave 40, then 4–5 |
| Decoration | from wave 26: 0.5% per wave past 25 |
| Rare-hued item | from wave 26: 1% per wave past 25 |
| Rare-hued cloth | from wave 31: 0.5% per wave past 30 |
| **Gauntlet artifact** | from wave 36: 0.5% per wave past 35. One of: Hunter's Headdress, Spirit of the Totem, Tangle, Hat of the Magi, Paladin Cloak |
| **PvM rune** | 5% per 10 waves cleared (not on free-entry stones) |

## Leaderboard

The stone keeps a leaderboard of runs: best wave, best gold and total runs.
