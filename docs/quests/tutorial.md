# New Player Tutorial

New characters get a guided start: you pick your skills and stats, then the **Adventurer's Quest Log** walks you through the shard with small quests that pay gold, items and reward points. Finish enough of them and you earn your first home: a **tent**.

## Creating your character

When you first log in you choose:

- **7 skills** from the Combat, Magic, Crafting and other categories.
- **Your stats**: exactly **150 points** split between Strength, Dexterity and Intelligence, each between **20 and 100** (50 / 50 / 50 to start).

| Stat | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Strength** | Increases your hitpoints and weapon damage |
| **Dexterity** | Increases your weapon attack speed |
| **Intelligence** | Increases your mana pool and your spell efficiency |

Some choices come with starter gear. For example, Ninjitsu starts with a katana, 100 bandages and 10 lesser heal potions.

## The Adventurer's Quest Log

The quest log is placed in your backpack once you confirm your skills and stats.

- Type **[Tutorial** to open it at any time.
- Quests are sorted in three tabs: **Exploration**, **Monster Hunts** and **Life Skills**.
- Progress counts by itself. There's no NPC to talk to, and each reward is given the moment a quest is finished.
- The **hotbar** (button at the bottom of the log) shows your active quests in a small window.
- When every quest is done, the log congratulates you and removes itself.

## The special reward: your first tent

Every quest also gives **tutorial XP**, shown as **+X XP** next to its name. The bottom of the quest log shows your progress towards a special reward.

- Quick tasks give **2 XP**, longer ones **5 to 15 XP**. Monster hunts give **50% more**: **8 to 23 XP**.
- At **150 XP** (out of about 395 for every quest) you receive a **blessed tent deed**, Blue or Green, in your **bank box**.
- A window explains your new home, and the quest log shows that the reward is waiting in your bank.

About the tent:

- It's a mere tent: canvas walls aren't the safest house in the realm, so don't keep your greatest treasures in it. But it's a perfect start for a beginner, with room to **secure** and **lock down** your first belongings.
- **Placing it:** double-click the deed and pick a spot of **flat ground** (8 × 8 tiles), not obstructed by rocks, trees or debris.
- Unlike other houses, a tent can be placed by **Young** players and before **40 hours** of play time.
- It still counts as your account's house.
- **Demolishing** it refunds 80% of its value to your bank box, like any other house.

!!! note
    House deeds, tents included, can't be sold to vendors.

## Help menu

Type **[Help** for the help menu, made for new players:

- **Find Closest Healer / Banker** and **Find Newbie Dungeon** show a tracking arrow to follow.
- A **Getting Started Guide**, quick tips and the list of [player commands](../core/commands.md).
- A link to the Compendium and to our Discord.
- **Seek Staff Member Assistance**, and **Teleport to Safety** if you're stuck.
- A **Starter Quests** button while your quest log isn't finished.
