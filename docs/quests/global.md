# Global Quests

Server-wide community goals. The whole shard works together on one objective, and everyone who helped is rewarded by how much they contributed.

## How it works

- A new global quest starts automatically. Talk to the **Global Quest NPC** to see the current goal, its progress and your contribution.
- Each quest lasts **3 hours**.
- When a quest ends, the next one starts once the full 3-hour window has passed.
- Every **daily quest** anyone completes takes **10 minutes** off the wait for the next global quest.

## Rewards

Your contribution is how much of the target **you** personally did.

```
reward = maximum pool × (your contribution / target)
```

| Quest type | Max reward points | Max monthly points | Max gold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Boss Hunt | 150 | 750 | 100,000 |
| Kill Specific Creature | 130 | 650 | 80,000 |
| Kill Monsters | 120 | 600 | 75,000 |
| Donate Magical Items | 110 | 550 | 55,000 |
| Harvest Resources | 100 | 500 | 50,000 |
| Craft Items | 90 (artificer points) | 450 | 45,000 |
| Donate Items | 90 | 350 | 35,000 |
| Destroy Items | 80 | 400 | 40,000 |

- You need at least **5% of the total community contribution** to get anything.
- Gold arrives as a **bank check** in your bank box.
- The **top 3 contributors** of a successful quest get a special extra reward.

### Failed quests

If time runs out, you're still rewarded at **half rate**, but only if the community reached at least **50% of the target**.
