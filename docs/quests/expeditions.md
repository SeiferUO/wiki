# Expeditions (Rifts)

Expeditions are **30-minute private mini-dungeons** behind a rift portal. Kill enough monsters and a **guardian** appears. Guardians carry most of the treasure, including exclusive items.

## Starting an expedition

Talk to **Samael, the Expedition Master**, in Britain, or say **[Expedition**.

1. Pick a **difficulty** and pay with **Achievement Points** or **Expedition Tickets**:

| Difficulty | Achievement Points | or Expedition Tickets |
|---|---|---|
| Easy | 200 | 2 |
| Medium | 300 | 4 |
| Hard | 400 | 6 |
| Elite | 500 | 8 |

2. A random rift of that difficulty opens at your feet. Each rift can only be open once at a time; if none is free, try again later.

You can only open a rift from a **town, safe zone or house**, and not while you're busy (casting, fighting).

## Who can enter

- The summoner, their **party** and their **guild**.
- Only **one character per IP** can be inside expeditions at a time (VIP accounts excepted).
- Stepping through shows a warning first.

## Inside

- The rift lasts **30 minutes**, then everyone inside is sent back out. Player corpses left inside are moved to the exit.
- Monsters keep respawning while players are inside.
- **Guardian gauge:** every monster you kill fills the gauge by its HP (100–1,000 per kill). At **15,000** the **guardian** spawns.
- No Recall, Gate Travel, Sacred Journey, Mark, Wall of Stone or Energy Field. No houses.
- Logging out inside takes **10 minutes**.
- An exit portal inside takes you back.

!!! danger "Lawless rifts"
    A rift opened inside a **[Danger Zone](../core/danger-zones.md)** is **lawless**: there are no combat restrictions inside, and anyone can be attacked and looted without penalty. The server announces it.

## Rifts

| Difficulty | Guardians |
|---|---|
| **Easy** | Norg, Baracoon, Stygian Drake, Mephitis, the Skeleton King |
| **Medium** | Blood Lurker, Molten Daemon, Eldritch Daemon, Rai Jin, Forest Spirit, Gargantua, Ancient Wyvern, Anima, The Undying, Shadowlord, the Bog Thing, the Forest Elder |
| **Hard** | Crimson King, Rai Jin, Malhazard, Malcanthet, Ulric the Damned, Semidar, Medusa |
| **Elite** | Medusa, Minax (Destard), the Frozen Ancestor (Glacia), Drakus, the Gorgon |

The portal shows the guardian's name and difficulty. Some portals show a different difficulty than the tier they come from.

## Expedition Tickets

An **Expedition Ticket** lets you start an expedition without spending Achievement Points. Tickets also shorten the [Doom champion](../bosses/champions.md#the-stygian-champion-doom) timer by 1 hour, and they show up as [rare loot](../items/rare-loot.md).
