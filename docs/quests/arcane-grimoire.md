# Arcane Grimoire

A quest for grandmaster mages. **Ilsandra, the Arcane Scholar** in Britain, sells **Unbound Grimoires**. Fill one with the damage your spells deal to monsters, and it lets you permanently empower one spell of your choice.

> *"An unbound grimoire hungers, mage. Feed it the agony your spells carve into the beasts of this realm. When its last page is full, it will bind to your will and etch one spell deeper into your soul... forever."*

## Getting a grimoire

Double-click Ilsandra to open the quest window. To get a grimoire you need:

| Requirement | |
|---|---|
| Magery | 100 |
| Evaluating Intelligence | 100 |
| Reward points | **50**, spent when you buy it |
| Grimoires in progress | none on your account |

Double-click the grimoire to **bind** it. A bound grimoire is **blessed** and tied to you: it can't be traded. **Only one grimoire per account** can gain progress at a time.

Ilsandra is also the arcanist: her window has a reward point shop and her mage goods (scrolls and spellbooks). You can also say *shop* or *vendor buy*.

## Filling it

- The grimoire records the damage **your spells** deal to **monsters**. The progress is added when the monster dies, and only if you have **looting rights** on it.
- **Summons count too.** Damage dealt by your summons is added, divided by the summon's control slots.
- Each monster can give at most as much as its full health, so healing monsters can't be farmed.
- Pets, summons, players and training dummies don't count.
- In **Haven**, progress is **halved**.
- There's **no time limit**.
- Double-click the grimoire to see your progress. Each kill shows how much it added.

## The reward

When the last page is full, the grimoire is **bound**. Double-click it and **choose a spell to empower**: **+1 arcane knowledge** for that spell, which makes it **1% more efficient**, up to **100**. This is the same knowledge as from arcane scrolls (see [Spell scrolls bonus](../magic/grimoire.md#spell-scrolls-bonus)).

Spells you can empower:

| Damage | Summons and others |
|---|---|
| Magic Arrow, Harm, Fireball, Lightning, Mind Blast, Energy Bolt, Explosion, Flame Strike, Poison, Paralyze, Mana Drain | Air, Earth, Fire and Water Elementals, Daemon, Summon Creature, Blade Spirits, Energy Vortex, Create Food, Greater Heal |

- Spells already at 100 aren't offered.
- If every spell is already at 100, your 50 reward points are returned.
- Then speak to Ilsandra for a new grimoire: the quest can be repeated.
