# Cigal's Mystery Creatures

**Cigal** is an animal trainer with a challenge for grandmaster tamers: take one of his **mystery creatures**, train it to its full potential within 72 hours, and earn an **Animal Taming or Animal Lore power scroll**.

## Starting the quest

Double-click Cigal to open the quest window. You need:

| Requirement | |
|---|---|
| Animal Taming | 100 |
| Animal Lore | 100 |
| Followers | **none** (stable your pets first) |
| Achievement Points | **250**, spent when you accept |

Accept and a mystery creature appears beside you, already **bonded** and following you. It can't be transferred or shared with friends.

## The creature

Every mystery creature is rolled at random:

- **Look:** one of 30 creature bodies, hued by its type.
- **Type:** Molten, Blood, Lightning, Cold, Poison, Death, Mystic or Earth.
- **Control slots:** 1, 2 or 3. More slots means more stats, HP, damage and armor.
- **Style:** a **mage** (more Intelligence, Magery, Eval Int and Meditation) or a **melee** fighter (more Strength, Dexterity and HP).
- **Skills** equal to your Animal Taming.
- **1 passive** and **1 ability per control slot** (up to 3), picked from its type's list.

Hover over the creature to see its type, XP, time left, abilities and passive.

### Abilities

- Abilities only fire against **monsters**, and not while the creature is below 10% HP.
- Each ability triggers on melee, on being hit or on spells, depending on the ability.
- **Chance:** 15% + 5% per stage. **Cooldown:** 35 s − 2 s per stage.
- The creature's **stage** goes up every 20,000 XP, up to stage 5 (40% chance, 25 s cooldown).

## Training it

The creature needs **100,000 XP**. It earns XP whenever **you or any of your pets** kill a monster:

- XP = the monster's max HP, up to **3,500 per kill**.
- Monsters with half the creature's HP or less give only **a quarter**.
- In the **Haven** dungeon, XP is halved.
- Kills in the Britain Zoo don't count, and you must be able to earn XP (see [Experience](../core/experience.md)).

You see your progress after every kill as *[XP / 100000 xp]*.

!!! warning "72-hour limit"
    You have **72 hours** from accepting the quest. If time runs out, the creature disappears and the quest fails. The 250 Achievement Points aren't refunded.

## Reward

When the creature reaches 100,000 XP it **vanishes** and you receive:

- a **+1 Animal Taming** or **+1 Animal Lore** power scroll (50/50), placed in your **bank box**. It raises that skill's cap by 1, up to 120.
- **monthly ranking points:** 500 per control slot.
