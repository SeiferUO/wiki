# Shepherd's Crook & Herding

**Herding** is a tamer support skill. It makes your pets hit harder, earns your pets more XP and gives you more stable slots. The **Shepherd's Crook** adds its own bonus on top and carries special pet properties.

## What Herding does

| Effect | Amount |
|---|---|
| **Pet damage** (melee and spells) | +Herding ÷ 8 % (15% at 120). Needs **more than 50 Herding**, and the pet within 16 tiles of you |
| **Crook bonus** | adds the crook's own bonus (below) to the same pet damage, if you hold a crook |
| **Pet XP** | +0.25% per point above 100 in Animal Taming, Animal Lore and Herding (up to +15% with all three at 120) |
| **Stable slots** | +1 at 40, +2 at 60, +3 at 80, +4 at 100, +5 at 120 Herding at the Animal Trainer |

- The damage bonus applies when your pets fight monsters (spells against any creature).
- **Herding Efficiency** (Unchained pet mastery) multiplies a pet's Herding bonus by +2.5% / +5% / +7.5% per tier. See [Unchained Pets](unchained.md).
- **Gaining Herding:** you get a skill check each time one of your pets makes a melee attack while you hold a crook and stand within 12 tiles of it.

## The crook bonus

Your crook's bonus (shown on it as *Herding Maximum Damage Bonus*, together with your Herding part):

| Source | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Damage level (loot crooks): Ruin / Might / Force / Power / Vanquishing | +2 / +4 / +6 / +8 / +10% |
| Wood: Oak / Ash / Yew / Heartwood / Bloodwood / Frostwood | +2 / +4 / +6 / +8 / +10 / +12% |
| Player-crafted Exceptional | +5% |
| Player-crafted Mastercrafted | +7% |

A Frostwood Mastercrafted crook gives +19%. With 120 Herding that's **+34%** pet damage in total. When you equip the crook you get a message showing your total.

## Fighting with a crook

- With **50+ Animal Taming or Herding**, the crook uses your **Wrestling** skill instead of Macing.
- The crook can't use weapon special moves. Many warrior bonuses (masteries, codex, Death rage) don't apply to it.
- Holding a crook **blocks the Mage mastery ability**.
- With **Aero** mastery, your pets get its attack speed bonus only while you hold a crook or fight bare-handed.
- Equipping a crook while shapeshifted ends your **Druidic** stance.

## Crook properties

Crooks roll their own pet properties (creature spell, melee and ability damage, resistances, veterinary boost and more). See [Gear Properties](../items/gear-properties.md#shepherds-crook) for the list and caps, and [Enchanting & Crafting](../items/enchanting-crafting.md) for crafting and Shepherd's Crook enhancement scrolls.

## Durability

- When you damage a monster outside PvP, your crook has a chance to lose 1 durability *(set by staff)*, at most once per cooldown *(set by staff)*.
- **Arms Lore** protects it: 2% per 10 points. A **Danger Zone** Fortification bonus adds up to 15% more.
- With the **Prevent Destruction** option on, a crook at 1 durability is moved to your backpack instead of breaking.
- Crook properties wear down as your pets deal damage (see [Enchanting & Crafting](../items/enchanting-crafting.md)).

## Crook styles

Equip a crook and use **Change Style** in its context menu.

- Unlock a style for **10 Platinum Coins**. Random Transmog Deeds can also unlock one.
- The style applies to any crook you equip. It turns back to the normal look when you unequip it or enter PvP combat.
- The **Disable Transmog** option turns styles off.
