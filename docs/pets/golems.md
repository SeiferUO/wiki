# Golems

Tinkers can build a **golem**: a mechanical companion that levels up, adapts to your mastery and overheats into overdrive.

## Building a golem

Double-click a **Golem Assembly Kit** in your backpack. You need:

- **100 Tinkering**,
- **200 combined points** in Tinkering, Item Identification, Remove Trap and Detect Hidden,
- **4 free control slots**.

You can't assemble one in PvP. Controlling the golem afterwards needs the same 200 combined points (see [Taming](../skills/taming.md#golems)).

!!! warning
    **Releasing a golem destroys it.**

Your golem's abilities and passive come from **your active mastery**. If you change mastery, the golem changes too.

|---|---|---|

| Earth | Double Strike, Crushing Blow, Shatter | More damage to paralyzed or frozen foes |
| Aero | Tempest, Shockwave | Cooldowns tick down faster |
| Blood | Blood Syphon, Bleeding Strike | Regenerates HP in combat |
| Doom | Death Spray, Massacre, Blast Radius | Extra melee damage every 5 hits |
| Bulwark | Line Blast, Reflect, Double Strike | Up to 25% less damage taken |
| Nature | Root, Healing Pulse | Defensive stance when standing still |
| Lyrical | Pacify, Ethereal Sap | More damage to barded monsters |

| Poison | Poison Strike, Infectious Strike, Poison Cloud | Overheats from poison traps and poison spells |
| Shadow | Vanish, Enrage, Blind | Can hide and stealth |
| Holy | Sacrifice, Concentration, Holy Light | 25% chance to reflect spells |
| Death | Wither, Massacre, Enrage | Up to +25% damage below 60% HP |

| Oblivion | Runic Curse, Mana Leech | Mana shield when it's about to be destroyed |

## Heat and overdrive

Dealing and taking damage raises the golem's **heat**. At max heat it **overdrives** and becomes much stronger for a while. Some masteries also heat it from traps and spells.

## Leveling

The golem earns XP while you kill monsters. Each **stage** gives:

- **25 stat points** to spend on its stats,
- **3 passive points** for the passive tree.

Golems level up to **stage 10** normally.

### Passive tree

| Passive | Cost | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Unstoppable Force | 2 | +10% damage |
| Unlimited Knowledge | 2 | +10% to all skills |
| Overdrive | 3 | +25% overheat potency |
| Indestructible | 3 | −25% durability loss |
| Bulwark Stance | 5 | May turn invulnerable for a moment when hit |
| Savagery | 6 | +25% physical damage |
| Magical Potency | 7 | Learns Magery, Eval Int and Meditation |
| Limitless | 8 | Longer overheat |
| Magic Shield | 9 | May reflect 25% of incoming spells |
| Quicksilver | 10 | Bursts of attack speed |

That's 55 points for everything.

### Advanced levels (stages 11–20)

Feed **power scrolls** into a **Golem Power Core** to raise the stage cap up to **20**. Each advanced level needs the previous one and gives a bonus:

| Bonus |
|---|
| +10% ability cooldown reduction |
| +5 physical and spell resistance |
| +15% heat generation |
| +10% melee and spell damage |
| −10% durability loss |
| +10% special ability damage |
| +15% overheat ability damage |
| 15% chance to halve a cooldown |

## Stances

| Stance | Behavior |
|---|---|
| Ranged | Stays back and uses ranged attacks |
| Aggressive | Fights in close range |
| Defensive | Taunts enemies to draw their attacks |

## Cores

Install one **core** into your golem (remove the old one first). Cores have **integrity** (0–100) and stop working when depleted.

| Core | Effect |
|---|---|
| Core of Reflection | +25% chance to reflect spells |
| Core of Genesis | Passive HP regeneration |
| Core of Savagery | More melee damage |
| Core of Quickness | Faster attacks |

## Repairs

Golems lose durability in combat. **Golem Repair Kits** are crafted by tinkers. Using one needs 100 Tinkering.

- A kit repairs every damaged golem near you. It can also **resurrect** them.
- Kits hold up to **1,000 charges**. Combine kits of the same quality and material by targeting one with another.

Golems take half healing from spells.
