# Unchained Pet List

All Unchained pets by type. The table gives a quick overview; open **Ability details** under each type for the full descriptions.

- **Slots**: "1/2" means it's rolled at random when the pet spawns.
- **Innate**: the pet gets that [mastery](unchained.md#masteries) at +1 tier for free.
- **Hybrids** (marked with a second type) can use relics from both lineages.
- ★ = event pet.

    **Ash Unicorn**

    - *Solar Flare*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to trigger a fire explosion in a 3-tile radius hitting up to 4 enemies (20s cooldown).
    - *Firestrike*: 15–45% chance per melee hit to apply burning and deal 25–75% bonus fire damage.
    - *Pyromaniac* (passive): Incoming fire spells (Fireball, Explosion, Flamestrike) heal the unicorn instead of dealing damage, converting 25–75% of the damage to HP.

    **Ashen Muck**

    - *Magma Crash*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to slam the target area with two expanding lava rings hitting up to 3 nearby enemies, applying burning to all targets (20s cooldown).
    - *Molten Shell* (passive): Incoming fire spells (Fireball, Explosion, Flamestrike) heal the muck for 15–40% of the damage they would have dealt instead of dealing damage (scales with stage).

    **Bonfire Wisp**

    - *Fire Damage*: 10–40% chance on melee or spell hit to apply burning and deal 20–75% bonus fire damage.
    - *Cinder Shell* (passive): 15–40% chance when struck in melee to reflect the full melee damage back to the attacker (8s cooldown).

    **Ember Beetle**

    - *Eruption*: 10–35% chance on melee hit or when taking damage to release an AoE explosion in a 3-tile radius hitting up to 3 enemies (15s cooldown).
    - *Flamestrike Burst* (passive): 15–45% chance per melee hit to deal bonus fire damage and apply burning to the target (10s cooldown).

    **Emberfrog**

    - *Immolate Reaction*: 10–40% chance when taking damage from a monster to retaliate with a fire damage burst and apply burning to the attacker.
    - *Flamestrike* (passive): 25–55% chance per melee hit to deal bonus fire damage and apply burning to the target (10s cooldown).

    **Fire Scorpion**

    - *Scorching Sting*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to deal bonus fire damage, set the target burning, and apply a burn DoT via ApplyBurn (20s cooldown).
    - *Molten Carapace Burst* (passive): 15% max chance when taking damage to erupt in lava rings hitting up to 4 nearby enemies and applying burning to all targets (15s cooldown).

    **Firemane**

    - *Firebreath*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to spray 40–60 projectiles in a wide cone hitting up to 3 targets (15s cooldown).
    - *Inferno Charge* (passive): Movement builds fire charges (max 100); at full charge the next attack triggers Inferno Ready. Passively grants +20% damage against burning targets.

    **Hell Hound**

    - *Firebreath*: Sprays 40–60 fire projectiles in a wide cone hitting up to 3 targets (15s cooldown).
    - *Infernal Strikes* (passive): Every 10th melee hit triggers 1–4 rapid follow-up strikes scaled by stage (capped at 4× multiplier).

    **Hellrazor**

    - *Spike Fend*: Every 15th melee hit triggers an AoE spike explosion in a 6-tile radius (up to 3 targets) released in 5 waves with 500ms delays.
    - *Cinder Shell* (passive): 15–40% chance when struck in melee to reflect the full melee damage back to the attacker (8s cooldown).

    **Infernal Gazer**

    - *Meteor Swarm*: Calls down 2–4 meteors per tick over 3–10 ticks (scales with stage); each meteor hits up to 3 targets on impact.
    - *Infernal Surge* (passive): When hit by a fire spell, boosts spell damage by 25–50% and applies burning to the attacker (10s cooldown).

    **Infernal Imp**

    - *Fire Burst*: 10–40% chance on melee hit, spell hit, or when taking damage to deal bonus fire damage and apply burning (10s cooldown).
    - *Hellfire Conduit* (passive): 10–40% chance per spell hit to apply burning to the target.

    **Lava Lizard**

    - *Stygian Burst*: Fires 15–30+Stage lava fireballs in a radial pattern creating 2–4 lava fields; AoE damage triggers in a 3-tile radius at impact after a delay.
    - *Molten Scales* (passive): Incoming fire spells heal the lava lizard for 15–35% of the damage they would have dealt (scales with stage).

    **Manticore**

    - *Dragon Breath*: On melee hit, fires a cone attack up to 6 tiles, applying poison and dealing damage that scales by 1.0+0.1×Stage.
    - *Sanguine Flame* (passive): Incoming fire spells heal the manticore for 35–75% of the damage they would have dealt.

    **Molten Crawler**

    - *Charge*: Dashes toward the target, dealing damage to anything in the path and releasing a 3-tile AoE slam at the destination, leaving lava fields (15s cooldown).
    - *Firestrike* (passive): 20–50% chance per melee hit to apply burning to the target (10s cooldown).

    **Molten Drake**

    - *Molten Flames Beam*: 25% max chance to fire a directional beam of fire projectiles up to 8 tiles, leaving fire fields and dealing damage to all creatures hit (20s cooldown).
    - *Scorching Strikes* (passive): If the target is burning: deals 5+Stage×3% bonus damage and extinguishes the fire. If not burning: 10+Stage% chance to ignite the target (5s cooldown).

    **Molten Dryad**

    - *Burning Blossom*: On spell hit, scatters ignited petals onto the primary target and up to 3 nearby enemies, applying burning (20s cooldown).
    - *Sanguine Flame* (passive): Fire spells heal the dryad for 35–75% of the spell damage dealt.

    **Molten Harpy**

    - *Feather Wing Burst*: 25% max chance on melee hit to release 5 waves of feather AoE attacks (500ms apart) hitting up to 5 targets within 6 tiles; each wave deals 25% less than the previous (15s cooldown).
    - *Infernal Shielding* (passive): 25% max chance on taking damage from a monster to activate an infernal shield for 10 seconds (20s cooldown).

    **Molten Mongbat**

    - *Firestrike*: Stage×5% chance per melee hit to apply burning to the target (10s cooldown).
    - *Pyromaniac* (passive): Incoming fire spells (Fireball, Explosion, Flamestrike) heal the mongbat for 25+Stage×5% of the damage they would have dealt (max 75%).

    **Phoenix**

    - *Fireball Volley*: 25% max chance on melee or spell hit to fire 3–6 fireballs (scales with stage) creating fire fields on impact (20s cooldown).
    - *Phoenix Revival* (passive): When near death, revives once with 10+Stage×5% HP (max 60%) if the 10-minute cooldown has elapsed.

    **Saberdrake**

    - *Fireball Barrage*: Fires 5–10+Stage fireballs in 4 strike waves over 0.6 seconds; the first hit deals 1.5× damage; 35% chance to create fire fields on impact (15–20s cooldown).
    - *Burning Touch* (passive): 5 strikes on the same target will ignite and damage the enemy.

    **Volcanic Vollem**

    - *Magma Shell*: Every 5 melee hits erupts in lava rings, deals 10% of the target's max HP as fire damage, applies a 5-tick burn DoT, and splashes to 4 nearby enemies in a 3-tile radius (18s cooldown).
    - *Molten Resolve* (passive): Stacks +3% damage bonus every 30 seconds while alive (max +30%). Decays 3% per 30s out of combat. Resets on death.

    **Young Arachdrake**

    - *Brood Genesis*: 25% chance on melee hit to spawn 1–3 spiderling broodlings (max 5 total; 30s cooldown). Consuming an injured broodling (<30% HP) heals self for 2× their current HP.
    - *Nature Selection* (passive): Chances to devour weak broodlings to heal itself with a possibility of spawn another spiderling.

    **Young Molten Dragon**

    - *Infernal AoE*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to erupt in 3 rings of fire, applying burning to the primary and splashing to up to 3 nearby enemies with reduced damage (20s cooldown).
    - *Infernal Shield* (passive): 25% chance when taking 5+ damage to raise a 5-second damage absorption shield on self (15s cooldown).

    **Aero Flesh Renderer**

    - *Protection Bubble*: 20% chance on melee hit (45s cooldown) to form a protective bubble granting 100% resistance to all damage for 4+Stage×0.2 seconds.
    - *Massacre* (passive): Every 3 melee hits triggers a Massacre dealing bonus bleed damage to the target.

    **Aero Phoenix**

    - *Gale Push*: 25% max chance on spell or melee hit to push the target 2+Stage/3 tiles away (up to 6); wall collisions deal up to 2× damage bonus (10–25s cooldown).
    - *Aero Surge* (passive): Each spell hit adds 0.005+Stage×0.0025 to CastingSpeedBonus; at 0.3 it resets with a visual burst.

    **Bladetail Lizard**

    - *Spine Strike*: 25% chance on melee hit to launch 3–12 projectiles in a high arc; each deals scaled damage, and the final impact applies a fire DoT (15–20s cooldown).
    - *Life Sap* (passive): Chance on melee hit to drain health points from the target and heal self (8s cooldown).

    **Cave Dweller**

    - *Slam Charge*: 25% chance on melee to push the target 2–6 tiles; deals bonus damage plus 50–100% extra if the target hits a wall (10–25s cooldown, scales with stage).
    - *Bulwark Shield* (passive): 25% chance on melee or taking damage to grant a 50–200 total protection shield split between self and nearby allies (cooldown scales with stage).

    **Chesapeake Blue Crab**

    - *Pinch*: Freezes the target for 2 seconds (15s cooldown).
    - *Stamina Regen* (passive): Restores 2+Stage/3 stamina per step. Deals 10–30% bonus damage against paralyzed, frozen, or movement-impaired targets.

    **Chillmuck**

    - *Slow*: 25% max chance to slow the target's movement for 5–7.5 seconds (25s cooldown).
    - *Crushing Blow* (passive): Every 5 hits triggers a heavy strike dealing bonus damage.

    **Cold Drake**

    - *Crystallization*: Every 10 melee hits fires multi-stage projectiles in 3–5 iterations (1s apart) hitting up to 5 targets in a 6-tile radius; each tick decays by 25%.
    - *Protection Shield* (passive): 25% chance when struck to restore 50+Stage×15 HP to the tamer's protection shield (max 200).

    **Frostfang**

    - *Berserk*: When the attack speed bonus cap is reached, triggers a 3–5s berserk with +30% bonus damage (30s cooldown).
    - *Glacial Shroud* (passive): 25% max chance on incoming spell to reflect it and gain +2.5% attack speed stacking toward the 30% cap (10s cooldown).

    **Galewing**

    - *Air Barrage*: 25% max chance on melee hit to fire 7–10 projectiles in a scatter pattern hitting up to 3 nearby monsters (20s cooldown).
    - *Gale Force* (passive): Movement builds wind charges (max 100); at full charge the next hit freezes the target for 1.5 seconds and triggers a 3-tick damage burst.

    **Giant Frog**

    - *Croak*: Pacifies small HP creatures for a duration scaling with Peacemaking skill; damages larger creatures outright (15s cooldown).
    - *Regeneration* (passive): Continuously heals 2.5–15% of max HP every 10–15 seconds; rate is faster when out of combat.

    **Glacial Ox**

    - *Charge*: Every 15 seconds, dashes toward the target dealing DamageMax physical damage and freezing all enemies in its path for 2 seconds; AoE hit in a 3-tile radius at the destination (Stage 6+: releases a cryo ring on impact).
    - *Slow* (passive): 25% max chance on melee hit to slow the target's movement for 5–7.5 seconds (15s cooldown).

    **Gold Grubber**

    - *Fortune Strike*: 8–30% chance on melee hit to force the target to drop gold equal to HitsMax/10 × (1+5%/stage), up to 150 gold (10–25s cooldown).
    - *Critical Strike* (passive): 10–35% chance per melee hit to deal 15–45% bonus physical damage (5–15s cooldown).

    **Jellyfish**

    - *Electric Burst*: If the target is not frozen: freezes for 2s and deals damage. If the target is already frozen: deals 2× damage (20s cooldown).
    - *Venom Touch* (passive): 15+Stage×3% chance to apply poison on every melee and spell hit.

    **Nimble Cow**

    - *Double Strike*: Deals bonus physical damage on a follow-up melee hit (10s cooldown).
    - *Quicksilver* (passive): Grants self a +7.5–25% attack speed bonus for 10 seconds (25s cooldown).

    **Polar Bear**

    - *Freeze*: Every 6 melee hits, freezes the target for 0.5–2 seconds depending on the target's remaining HP.
    - *Corpse Devour* (passive): 10–25% chance every 10 seconds to consume a nearby corpse and restore 10–25% of max HP.

    **Sealings**

    - *Slap!*: 35% chance on melee hit to wildly slap up to 2 additional enemies within 2 tiles of the primary target (8s cooldown).
    - *Pack Healing* (passive): When healed by a spell, splits the same heal amount equally among all other Sealings belonging to the same tamer within 12 tiles.

    **Shiverfox**

    - *Sand Spit*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to spray 40–60 projectiles hitting up to 5 targets and applying a 10–15% movement slow for 5–10 seconds (25s cooldown).
    - *Static Surge* (passive): Movement builds lightning charges (max 100); at full charge the next melee hit or spell discharges a lightning strike on the target.

    **Snow Ant**

    - *Glacial Blast*: 10–40% chance on melee hit to fire a linear ice beam up to 12 tiles dealing damage to all creatures in its path (20s cooldown).
    - *Glacial Shield* (passive): 25% chance when struck to raise a 50–200 HP damage absorption shield on self (15s cooldown).

    **Storm Harpy**

    - *Storm Bolt*: 25% chance on melee hit to fire a linear lightning bolt up to 14 tiles through enemies (20s cooldown).
    - *Storm Fury* (passive): 10–35% chance on melee hit to gain a 5–25% attack speed and accuracy buff for 6–15 seconds (20s cooldown).

    **Triton**

    - *Regurgitate*: Fires 5 projectiles with 200–500ms staggered delays, applies poison to targets, and leaves a poison cloud trail (15–20s cooldown).
    - *Affliction Bonus* (passive): +10–30% bonus damage against targets that are bleeding, poisoned, or diseased.

    **Volticlaw**

    - *Static Breath*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to fire a ranged projectile barrage at a target within 12 tiles, hitting up to 3 enemies in the impact zone (20s cooldown).
    - *Static Fur* (passive): Absorbs incoming lightning spell damage and heals self for the full amount that would have been dealt.

    **Vorpal Drake**

    - *Multicast*: 10–35% chance on spell hit to instantly cast the same spell a second time at no mana cost (15s cooldown).
    - *Storm Conduit* (passive): 15–40% chance when hit to absorb the damage and convert 25+Stage×2% of it into mana (10s cooldown).

    **Walrus**

    - *Aero Cone*: 25% max chance on melee hit to release a cold breeze cone (2–5 tile radius) dealing 50% damage to enemies and healing the tamer and allies for 50% of total damage dealt (15s cooldown).
    - *Life Sap* (passive): 30% max chance on melee hit to drain 5+Stage×3 HP from the target and heal self for the same amount (8s cooldown).

    **Water Spirit**

    - *Water Breath Barrage*: 7.5–25% chance to spray 14–20 projectiles at a target within 12 tiles, dealing damage in a 5-tile impact radius (15–20s cooldown).
    - *Mana Restore* (passive): 25% chance on melee or spell hit to restore mana to up to 5 nearby allies within 6 tiles (priority: tamer > party > others, 20s cooldown).

    **White Wyrm**

    - *Freezing Aura*: Deals initial cold damage and applies a 5–7.5s slow; creates frost projectiles in a staggered burst (300ms apart) slowing up to 5 nearby monsters (15s cooldown).
    - *Glacial Shield* (passive): 25% chance when struck for 5+ damage to raise a protection shield of 50+Stage×15 HP on self (max 200, 15s cooldown).

    **Young Abysmal Horror**

    - *Ink Cone*: Every 5th melee hit releases a poison ink cone spray dealing damage and applying poison to up to 5 targets hit.
    - *Reflect Splash* (passive): 7–25% chance to reflect an incoming monster spell back at the caster with bonus damage (10s cooldown).

    **Blood Ant**

    - *Corpse Devour*: 10–25% chance every 10 seconds to consume a nearby corpse and restore 10–25% of max HP.
    - *Blood Feed* (passive): Every 5 hits when below 50% HP, heals for damage × Stage × 0.05 (up to 50% of damage dealt).

    **Blood Bear**

    - *Rending Swipe*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to deal bonus damage and heal 25–75% of the damage dealt; healing scales higher the more injured the bear is (15s cooldown).
    - *Devour* (passive): 25% chance every 10 seconds to consume a nearby corpse and restore 10–25% of max HP.

    **Blood Drake**

    - *Savage Frenzy*: Every 8 melee hits triggers a frenzy visual and applies a 5-tick bleed DoT (20s cooldown).
    - *Ramping Damage* (passive): Each consecutive melee hit stacks 5% bonus damage (max +30%); resets at 8 hits.

    **Blood Gazer**

    - *Hemorrhage Ray*: 10–35% chance on spell hit to fire a blood beam that drains HP from the target and heals the gazer for 40–80% of the damage dealt (20s cooldown).
    - *Siphon Blood*: 25% chance on melee hit to drain damage from 2–5 nearby enemies in a radius (scales with stage), healing the gazer proportionally (15s cooldown).
    - *Hemorrhage* (passive): 15–40% chance per melee or spell hit to apply a bleed DoT to the target.

    **Blood Grubber**

    - *Blood Explosion*: On death, detonates the accumulated blood pool (capped at 3× own max HP) split evenly among all monsters within 5 tiles as physical damage.
    - *CHUNK* (passive): On a lethal melee blow, absorbs 20% of the target's max HP as healing and stacks it into a blood pool for the death explosion; also grants +0.5 scalar damage bonus (cap 30).

    **Blood Sabertooth**

    - *Evasion Sap*: On evading an attack: chance to instantly counterstrike (ApplySwiftCounterstrike) AND drain 5+Stage×2 HP from the attacker, healing self for the same amount.
    - *Aggressor Fury* (passive): Melee damage scales with the number of nearby aggressors: +5% per aggressor, capped at +30%.

    **Blood Worm**

    - *Syphon Blood*: Fires a cone attack up to 6 tiles away, draining HP from all creatures hit (20s cooldown).
    - *Affliction Bonus* (passive): +10–30% bonus damage against targets that are bleeding, poisoned, or diseased.

    **Blood Wyrm**

    - *Blood Leech*: When near blood pools, absorbs up to 5 within a 5-tile range gaining 3–6% attack speed per pool absorbed (max 30%) for 5–10 seconds (25s cooldown).
    - *Bleeding* (passive): 20+Stage×2.5% chance per spell hit to apply a bleed DoT to the target (20s cooldown).

    **Bloodspine**

    - *Spine Volley*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to fire 20–30 blood-quill projectiles in a wide cone (±60°) hitting up to 3 enemies within 7 tiles; 15+Stage×2.5% chance to apply a 3-tick bleed DoT per target hit (20s cooldown).
    - *Lifesteal* (passive): 35% max chance on melee hit to heal self for the full amount of damage dealt (6s cooldown).

    **Bloodstrider**

    - *Bloodrage*: 35% max chance on melee hit to enter a bloodrage for 5–9.5 seconds, causing all hits to heal 5–15% of damage dealt (cooldown scales down with stage).
    - *Double Strike*: 10–30% chance on melee to immediately strike the target again; hits deal 1.5× damage during Bloodrage (3s cooldown).
    - *Melee Splash* (passive): 15–40% chance when surrounded by 2+ enemies to hit up to 2 additional enemies within 1 tile.

    **Bloodwarg**

    - *Berserk*: 25% max chance on melee hit to enter a berserk rage for 8–18 seconds, gaining +30% damage at the cost of reduced healing received (30s cooldown).
    - *Melee Splash* (passive): 15–40% chance when surrounded by 2+ enemies to hit up to 2 additional enemies within 1 tile.

    **Basilisk**

    - *Stone Spray*: 25% max chance on melee hit to fire stone shards in a cone 3–8 tiles long, hitting multiple enemies for 30–100% damage and applying a freeze (20s cooldown).
    - *Directional Bonus* (passive): +15–30% bonus damage to enemies that are not facing the basilisk.
    - *Directional Mitigation* (passive): 10–25% damage reduction from incoming melee and spell attacks made by enemies not facing the basilisk.

    **Brown Bear**

    - *Mauling*: Each consecutive melee hit stacks +5% bonus damage (max +30%). At the cap, freezes the target for 0.5 seconds and resets the counter.
    - *Endurance* (passive): When near-fatal damage is taken, survives with 25% HP and gains 25–50% magic and physical resistance for 5 seconds (once per 20 seconds).

    **Cave Bear**

    - *Bleed Attack*: Applies a bleed DoT to the target on melee hit (15s cooldown).
    - *Pack Diversity* (passive): Deals up to +40% bonus melee damage scaled by the number of distinct pet types controlled by the tamer.

    **Cave Kepetch**

    - *Predator Sense*: 25% max chance on melee hit to activate a timed mode (6–15s, 30s cooldown): heals 10–20% of raw damage on each hit and deals up to +35% bonus damage to Poisoned, Bleeding, or Diseased targets.
    - *Affliction Predator* (passive): +10–20% bonus damage against targets that are bleeding, diseased, or poisoned (15% base + 2% per stage, capped at 20%).

    **Dust Lurker**

    - *Pestilence Spray*: 25% max chance on melee hit to spray a poison cone (2–5 tile radius) applying Greater poison and dealing damage to all enemies hit (15s cooldown).
    - *Stone Shield* (passive): Every 10 hits received, raises a protection shield on the tamer for 75–125+Stage×10 HP (max 250, 20s cooldown).

    **Earth Wyrm**

    - *Seismic Slam*: Damages everything in front of the wyrm, the size of the slam increases with the wyrms stage.
    - *Earthwell Revival* (passive): When near death, revives once with 15+Stage×5% HP (max 25%) shield AND health - if the 10-minute cooldown has elapsed.

    **Earthcrawler**

    - *Crystallize*: 10–35% chance on melee hit to deal 5 ticks of damage in a 6-tile radius to up to 5 targets, each tick decaying by 25% (10–40s cooldown, scales with stage).
    - *Rocky Carapace* (passive): When struck, fires stone shards back at the attacker dealing 50–100% of the damage received as retaliation.

    **Forest Spirit**

    - *Forest Bloom*: Heals up to 4 nearby allies within 6 tiles: players heal 5–15% of max HP, pets heal 10–20% of max HP (10–30s cooldown, scales with stage).
    - *High HP Bonus* (passive): +15–16% bonus melee damage when the reaper is above 80% HP.

    **Giant Rat**

    - *Disease Reflection*: Reflects an incoming monster spell back at the caster and applies a 3-tick disease DoT on them (15s cooldown).
    - *Diseased Advantage* (passive): +15–30% bonus melee damage against targets that are diseased, bleeding, or poisoned.

    **Great Hart**

    - *Quicken*: 25% on melee / 5% on taking damage: gains 15–30% weapon accuracy and attack speed for 10 seconds and restores stamina (20s cooldown).
    - *Flanking* (passive): +15–27.5% bonus melee damage when the tamer is within range.

    **Great Plains Wolf**

    - *Pack Fury*: 25% max chance on melee hit to enter IsBerserk mode for 8–20 seconds (30s cooldown).
    - *Menagerie Alter* (passive): Outgoing melee damage gains +7% per unique UnchainedPet lineage controlled by the tamer nearby (up to +35%).

    **Grubber**

    - *Executioner Strike*: Deals 2× damage to full HP targets; bonus scales with the target's missing HP and can execute targets outright below 25% HP.
    - *Stamina Regeneration* (passive): Restores 5+Stage stamina per step while controlled.

    **Mountain Ox**

    - *Charge*: Dashes in a line toward the target dealing path damage, ending with a 3-tile AoE slam; grants the tamer 7.5–15% weapon damage and physical resistance for 10 seconds (15s cooldown).
    - *Warlord Fury* (passive): Melee damage increases by 8–8.8% for each additional enemy within 12 tiles (up to +75% cap).

    **Robust Bull**

    - *Taunt*: 5% chance when taking or dealing damage to force nearby enemies to target self and gain 25–50% physical and magic resistance for 7 seconds (20s cooldown).
    - *Ironclad Wrath* (passive): +5–30% bonus melee damage while HP is above 70% (scales with stage).

    **Sabertooth**

    - *Cleave*: 25% chance when surrounded to hit up to 3 nearby enemies in the same swing (15s cooldown).
    - *Execution Strike* (passive): Deals 2× damage when the target is below 30% HP and applies a DoT (20s cooldown).

    **Scarab Beetle**

    - *Carapace*: When taking damage, gains 10–30% magic and physical resistance for 15 seconds (30s cooldown).
    - *Pack Mule* (passive): Carries a StrongBackpack, providing expanded pack animal storage.

    **Squirrel**

    - *Bard Invigoration*: When the tamer's bard performs: 25–75% chance (scales with Stage) to restore 20–40% of max HP and fully refill stamina (20–45s cooldown).
    - *Berserker* (passive): 25% max chance on melee hit to enter IsBerserk mode for 8–20 seconds (30s cooldown).

    **Triceratops**

    - *Charge*: 35% max chance on combat to dash to the target, dealing 2× damage to everything in the path and at impact (15s cooldown).
    - *Cleave* (passive): 25% chance when surrounded by 2+ enemies to cleave and hit up to 3 nearby targets (10s cooldown).

    **Vollem**

    - *Special Attack*: Every 20 seconds, randomly executes one of: Paralyze (3s freeze), Poison (level scales with stage), Double Strike, or Bleed DoT.
    - *Damage Absorption* (passive): While within 2 tiles of the tamer, absorbs 5–20% of all incoming damage directed at the tamer.

    **Vulture**

    - *Corpse Devour*: 10–35% chance every 10 seconds to move to a nearby corpse, consume it (converting it to bones), and restore 10–25% of max HP.

    - *Bleed Attack*: Applies a bleed DoT to the target on melee hit (15s cooldown).
    - *Enrage* (passive): 25% on melee hit / 5% on taking damage: gains 15–30% weapon damage and attack speed for 10 seconds (20s cooldown).
