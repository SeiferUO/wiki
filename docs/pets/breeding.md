# Pet Breeding

Two **Unchained pets** can be bred at the **creature keeper** to produce a newborn. The parents are **consumed** by the breeding.

## Requirements

- Both parents are **stabled** with you.
- Both are at **stage 5** or higher.
- One **male** and one **female**.
- Their **lineages are compatible** (see below).
- Both have had **all relic abilities removed**.
- You have fewer than 3 followers out, so the newborn has room.

## Lineages

Every Unchained pet belongs to a lineage: **Molten, Aero, Blood, Death, Nova, Earth** or **Noxious**.
Every lineage can breed with every other lineage, **except** these two pairs:

- **Molten × Aero**
- **Blood × Noxious**

Use the breeding simulator in the creature keeper's window to check a pair before committing.

## Outcomes

Each breeding rolls one of these outcomes:

| Outcome | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Pure** | A new pet of one parent's species. Purity goes up by 2.5–7.5. 25% chance of **+1 control slot** (max 3). Damage, armor and poison resist get a **+5% to +15% genetic bonus** |
| **Hybrid** | One parent's species with **mixed lineage** from both parents |
| **Rare** | A **rare species** from the lineage pool (see below) |
| **Miracle** | 1% chance of a legendary **event creature** |

### Odds

Odds depend on the parents' average **blood purity** and control slots:

| Parents | Pure | Rare | Hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same species | 20% + purity × 0.4 (max 60%) | — | the rest |
| Same lineage, different species | 20% + purity × 0.4 | 5% + purity × 0.1, +2% per extra control slot | the rest |
| Different lineages | — | 5% + purity × 0.15, +2% per extra control slot | the rest |

A **rare parent** always produces a rare offspring.

## What the newborn inherits

| Trait | Inheritance |
|---|---|
| **Stats** | Takes each parent's best stat bonus × **75–90%** (at least 80% if both parents are 95%+ purity). Only the highest stat is kept. The other two are re-rolled low (3–15) |
| **Blood purity** | Average of both parents. **−0.5 to −2.5** if they're different species. Pure outcomes add 2.5–7.5 |
| **Skill caps** | 50–75% of both parents' caps above 100 (max +20 combined) carry over |
| **Mastery XP** | **All** mastery XP from both parents combined |
| **Hues** | Every dye either parent collected |

The newborn arrives **bonded** and following you.

## Dragons

Dragons and drakes use their own breeding:

- The hatchling inherits **elemental types** from both parents, and the median of their **arcane berries**.
- Dragons have a 25% chance to lose magery and become strong melee fighters.
- Drakes have a 25% chance to learn magery at the cost of 20% of their stats.
- Skill caps, hues and mastery XP carry over as above.
