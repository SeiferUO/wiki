# Eldritch Orb

The **Eldritch Orb** is a crystal ball you hold in your off hand. While you hold it, every spell you cast has a chance to cost **no mana**.

## How it works

- Hold the orb in your hand. You can still cast with it, meditate with it, and hold it alongside a one-handed weapon.
- Each spell you cast successfully has a chance to trigger the orb. On a trigger the orb **absorbs the spell's mana cost** and uses **1 charge**.
- Every cast that doesn't trigger adds **+1% Accumulated Potency**, up to **+25%**. It resets when the orb triggers. Your current bonus shows on the orb.
- **PvM only:** the orb doesn't trigger while you're in PvP combat.

The tooltip shows the orb's trigger chance (*% Chance of Mana Refund*), its charges and any accumulated potency.

## Potency

Each orb rolls its trigger chance and maximum charges when it's made:

| Potency | Trigger chance | Max charges |
|---|---|---|
| Potent | 1–5% | 25–50 |
| Surpassingly Potent | 6–10% | 50–100 |
| Eminently Potent | 11–15% | 125–150 |
| Exceedingly Potent | 16–20% | 125–200 |
| Supremely Potent | 21–25% | 125–250 |

## Getting one

- **Mage shops:** mages sell **Potent** orbs for 500 gold, fully charged.
- **Monster drops:** any monster can drop one. Mini bosses and dungeon bosses drop them far more often. Dropped orbs come with **25–50%** of their charges.

    | Monster | Possible potency |
    |---|---|
    | Below Hard tier | Potent |
    | Hard and Elite | Potent – Surpassingly |
    | Legendary | Potent – Eminently |
    | Mini bosses | Surpassingly – Exceedingly |
    | Dungeon bosses and stronger | Surpassingly – Supremely |

- **The Crimson Omen Orb**, from the [Crimson Courtship invasion](../quests/invasions.md): blessed, 25% trigger chance and 250 charges.

## Charges

!!! warning "Empty orbs crumble"
    When the last charge is used, the orb **crumbles to dust**, unless Auto-Recharge is on.

**Recharging:** use **Charge** in the orb's context menu, then target **Arcane Dust** in your backpack.

- Each charge costs **1 Arcane Dust**. The orb fills up as far as your dust allows.
- Each recharge **lowers the orb's max charges by 1**. When max charges reach 0, the orb crumbles.

**Auto-Recharge:** toggle it in the context menu.

- When the orb runs out, it refills itself, first from Arcane Dust items in your backpack, then from the Arcane Dust on your character.
- It also costs 1 max charge per refill.
- If you have no dust, the orb waits at 0 charges instead of crumbling.

## Other

- Insuring an orb costs a flat fee, like other [utility items](../core/player.md#insurance).
- An uninsured orb can be salvaged for Arcane Dust equal to its trigger chance.
