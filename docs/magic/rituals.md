# Summoner Rituals

Summoners use a **Summoner Lantern** to collect souls from corpses and release them as a **ritual** that empowers every summon they have out.

## The Summoner Lantern

Wield the lantern (one or two hands). It collects souls from fresh monster corpses **within 6 tiles**.

- **Spirit Speak** triggers a collection every time it succeeds. With *Auto-Recharge* on, the lantern collects by itself as you walk past corpses, at most once every 10 seconds.
- Each corpse can only be drained once. Corpses you'd be a criminal for touching don't count.
- The lantern holds **100 souls**. Souls fade by 5% every 15 seconds.
- Draining corpses also **extends the life** of your summons.

### Souls per corpse

| Creature tier | Souls |
|---|---|
| Below Hard | 1–2 |
| Hard | 2 |
| Elite | 4 |
| Legendary | 6 |
| Abomination | 8 |
| Mini-boss and above | 10 |

Lanterns made from better metal collect more:

| Metal | Souls | Empower bonus |
|---|---|---|
| Iron | ×1.0 | 5% |
| Dull Copper | ×1.1 | 7% |
| Shadow Iron | ×1.2 | 9% |
| Copper | ×1.3 | 10% |
| Bronze | ×1.4 | 11% |
| Gold | ×1.5 | 12% |
| Agapite | ×1.7 | 13% |
| Verite | ×1.9 | 14% |
| Valorite | ×2.2 | 14.5% |
| Azurite | ×2.5 | 15% |

At 100 souls the lantern can also **empower** your summons for 15 seconds. They get the bonus above (max 15%).

## Rituals

Choose one ritual type. At **100 souls** you can perform it (or let *Auto-Empower* do it). All souls are used up and **every summon you own** gets the effect.

Each ritual has its own XP, earned while it's your chosen ritual. You need **50 Spirit Speak** and a wielded lantern. XP caps at **200,000** per ritual.

| Ritual | Effect | How XP improves it |
|---|---|---|
| **Genesis** | Heals each summon based on how much mana **you** have left | 15% + XP/25,000 (max 50%) of the summon's HP × your mana % |
| **Timeless** | Extends each summon's duration | 1 + XP/20,000 minutes (max 10) |
| **Eldritch** | Gives empowered spells to caster summons | 1 + XP/30,000 spells (max 10) |
| **Call of the Sentinel** | Damage shield on each summon | 200 + XP/200 HP (max 750) |
| **Shared Mana** | Each summon gives you part of its mana | 5% + XP/20,000 % of its mana (max 25%) |
| **Quicksilver** | Faster attacks | 5 + XP/10,000 seconds (max 15) |
| **Savagery** | Summons go savage (+10% damage, +5% per control slot) | 10 + XP/20,000 seconds (max 20) |
| **Vigor** | Refills mana and stamina | — |

!!! tip "Mystical summons"
    Each ritual has a chance to turn a summon **Mystical** (a glowing hue and +10% damage against monsters).
    The chance is 1% per point of the summon's surge scalar, max 30%.

Rituals don't work while you're in PvP combat.
