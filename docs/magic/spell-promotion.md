# Spell Promotion

A rotation mini-game for pure mages. The game keeps asking you to cast a "promoted" spell. Casting the right one builds charges for bonus damage and gives you random extras.

## Who can use it

You need a **Wizard Mastery** item in your backpack and the pure mage skills:

| Requirement | Value |
|---|---|
| Casting skill | Magery **or** Necromancy 100 |
| Damage skill | Evaluating Intelligence 100 |
| Support skills | Focus + Meditation + Spirit Speak + Mysticism, **150 combined** |

It only works in PvM, and only with damaging spells.

## Setup

The first time you cast a damaging spell, a window asks you to pick **5 spells** for your rotation. Choose from:

- **Magery:** Magic Arrow, Harm, Poison, Fireball, Lightning, Mind Blast, Energy Bolt, Explosion, Flame Strike
- **Necromancy:** Mind Rot, Pain Spike, Poison Strike, Siphon Blood, Insect Swarm, Bone Cleave, Wither, Vampiric Embrace

You can change your rotation at any time from the toolbar.

## How it works

1. One of your 5 spells is shown as the **promoted spell**. Click its icon on the toolbar to cast it.
2. Cast the promoted spell and you:
    - get back **35% of its mana**,
    - gain **1 charge**,
    - get any bonus that was attached to it,
    - get a new promoted spell (never the same one twice in a row).
3. Cast any **other** damaging spell and your charges reset to 0.

### Charges

- Each charge adds **+10% PvM spell damage**.
- At **4 charges** your next spell **surges** for +25% damage, and the charges reset.

## Bonuses

Each new promoted spell has a **35% chance** to carry a bonus. It's shown as a gem on the toolbar.

| Bonus | Chance (when there is one) | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Cooldown | 25% | Spells cast **50% faster** |
| Lower Reagent Cost | 23% | Your next spell uses no reagents |
| Lower Mana Cost | 20% | Refunds the **full** mana cost of the spell |
| Weak Surge | 17% | A small surge |
| Regeneration | 11% | Heals you for the spell's mana cost over 5 seconds |
| Pixie | 4% | Summons a pixie helper (uses no control slots) |
