# Hellfire Rift

The **Hellfire Rift** is a PvP-enabled boss area. Clear its **15 abominations** to summon the **Abyssal Infernal**.

Say **[Hellfire** in game for a quick summary.

## When and where

- A new rift opens **every 8 hours**. The **Hellfire Event** board at Britain bank shows how long until the next one.
- **Hellfire Portals** (fiery red) open at the same time in three places:
    - the depths of **Deceit**,
    - **Destard**,
    - **Chaos Island**.
- Walk into a portal or double-click it and confirm to enter. **Exit Portals** (blue) inside take you back to **Britain**.

## Rules inside

!!! warning "PvP zone"
    The Hellfire realm is a **contested region**: anyone can attack you.

- You can't **Recall** or **Sacred Journey** out, and **Gate Travel** and **Mark** don't work.
- Logging out takes **10 minutes**. If you log out inside, you're moved back to town.
- When the rift resets, you get warnings 5 minutes, 1 minute, 30 seconds and 10 seconds before. Then everyone still inside is **evacuated** to safety and a fresh rift opens.

## The fight

1. **Kill the 15 abominations**: hellfire balrons, hellfire wyrms and hellfire berserkers. A **Remaining Minions** window shows how many are left.
2. When the last one dies, the **Abyssal Infernal** appears.
3. **15 minutes** after the Infernal dies, the entrance portals close until the next rift.

### Rewards

**Abominations:**

- 1% chance for an [artifact weapon](../items/artifacts.md),
- 5% chance for a mastery item.

**The Abyssal Infernal** is a mini boss and uses the shared [boss rewards](index.md#boss-rewards). It also has:

- a **10%** chance for a hellish decoration: skull totems, a daemon toy, an orange mushroom, a shrine sarcophagus, a blood pentagram, a lamppost, sparkling lava, a lava stone, a hellstone idol, melting menhirs and rocks, spear carcasses, a burning scarecrow, or the **Forge of the Elders**,
- a **1%** chance for an **Ethereal Cerberus** (10 charges) or a rare-hued dungeon cloth.

## Forge of the Elders

The **Forge of the Elders** upgrades ingots to the next tier, for half the amount:

- Copper → Dull Copper → Shadow Iron → Bronze → Gold → Agapite → Verite → Valorite.
- Double-click it and target ingots in your backpack. **2 ingots become 1** of the next tier.
- Up to **500** ingots at a time. The forge has **100 charges**.

Besides the Abyssal Infernal, it's also sold in the shop.
