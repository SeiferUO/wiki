# Doom Dungeon

The **Doom Dungeon** is a boss gauntlet. Your group fights through a series of Doom bosses, one after another, for some of the best loot in the game, including **Greater Doom armor**.

For the community Doom champion spawn, see [The Stygian Champion](champions.md#the-stygian-champion-doom). For the Doom global event, see [Global Events](../quests/global-events.md#the-sealed-gate-of-the-stygian-keep-doom).

## Getting in

The entrance is the **Doom Dungeon Portal**. You need a **Master Key** in your backpack.

**Master Keys** drop from mini bosses (2.5%) and dungeon bosses and stronger (5%) outside Doom, and come from some event rewards.

### Securing the portal

1. When the portal is free, use it with a Master Key. The key is used up and you **secure the portal for 30 minutes**.
2. **Party members within 10 tiles** are brought in with you.
3. While it's secured, only you, your **party** and your **guild** can enter, and each of them needs their own Master Key.
4. After 30 minutes the portal becomes **public for 30 minutes**: anyone can walk in, no key needed.
5. Then the portal **resets**: everyone inside is sent out and the bosses start over.

Fighters inside get a reminder every 5 minutes of how much time is left.

## The gauntlet

- Bosses are fought **one at a time**. Each room's doors stay locked until its boss falls, then the next boss wakes up.
- Every boss has its own special attacks (fire fields, webs, slime pools, pulls, meteor barrages and more), and some call in helpers.
- After the **last boss** dies, you have **5 minutes** before everyone is sent out and the dungeon resets.

## Rewards

Every boss drops:

- an **enhancement scroll**,
- **boss tokens**: special items, treasure maps, resources, magic items, a power scroll, runic components, orbs, mage scrolls, wands, imbuing materials and harvest maps, with a chance for pet items, hued items, arcane wool, a Book of Lost Knowledge and an Item Bless Deed,
- super boss loot.

When a boss falls you can also get:

| Reward | Chance |
|---|---|
| Bronze or Silver [PvM rune](../items/ankhs-runes.md) | 10% per boss |
| **Gold PvM rune** | 20% at the first boss, +20% per boss after; one per run |
| **Greater Doom armor piece** | 20% at the first boss, +20% per boss after; one per run |
| Recipe scroll | 5% per boss; one per run |

- Every player with looting rights earns Doom event points (1 per 300 damage, up to 25 per boss), and players who dealt 1,000+ damage get **100 Monthly Challenge points**.
- Say **[DoomDamage** to see the damage report of the last boss. It also opens 2 minutes after the kill.

## Doom armor sets

Doom sets are 6-piece armor sets (helm, gorget, arms, gloves, legs and chest). Each piece you wear adds to the set's bonus:

| Set | Bonus per piece |
|---|---|
| **Toxic** | +2.5% poison (poison damage and poison resistance) |
| **Rogue** | +2.5% stealth attack damage |
| **Undine** | +2.5% magic resistance |
| **Nightmare** | +2.5% weapon damage |
| **Bloodstained** | +1% life steal |
| **Lunar** | +2.5% attack speed |
| **Phoenix** | +2.5% fire damage reduction |
| **Magus** | +2.5% spell damage |
| **Ranger** | +1.25% pet damage |
| **Cleric** | +1.25% healing |
| **Wraithcaller** | +1.25% healing |
| **Daemon Bone** | +2% Hellfire Strike damage |

Each **Greater** piece counts **double** (a full Greater set gives twice the normal bonus). You can mix regular and Greater pieces of the same set.

**Where they drop:**

- **Doom sets:** Elite-tier and stronger paragons (5%), greater paragons (25%), paragon chests and Mythic monsters.
- **Greater Doom sets:** the Doom Dungeon bosses and [Peerless bosses](peerless.md).
