# The Harrower

The Harrower is summoned by a player who has collected all **8 Champion Idols** in a **Tome of the Harrower**.

## The Tome of the Harrower

- **Crafted** by a grandmaster scribe: Inscription 100, 3 **Books of Lost Knowledge**.
- Double-click it (in your backpack) to open the idol map. Click an idol's spot to add the matching **Champion Idol** from your backpack. Each idol added gives **5,000 monthly ranking points**.

### Champion Idols

| Idol | Dropped by |
|---|---|
| Sandspire | Sandspire (Sandstorm champion), 25% |
| Progenitor | Progenitor Dragon (Destard champion), 25% |
| Molten Wyrm | Molten Wyvern (Delucia champion), 25% |
| Terathan Queen | Terathan Queen (Shame champion), 25% |
| Frozen Wyrm | Frozen Wyrm (Glacia champion), 25% |
| Goliath | Goliath (Eventide champion), 25% |

| Doom | **Doombringer**, the last boss of Doom, 25% |

The [Earthsunderer](peerless.md#the-earthsunderer) always drops one random idol (any except Doom). See [Champion Spawns](champions.md) for the champions.

## Summoning

When all 8 idols are in, the gate in the middle of the map lights up. Click it and confirm:

- A gate to the **Harrower's Lair** opens at your feet and the **Tome is consumed**.
- Only **one** Harrower gate can exist at a time.
- You can't do it on Chaos Island.

**Private hour:** for **60 minutes**, only you, your party and your guild can enter. The gate shows who summoned it and how long it stays private.

## The invasion

If the Harrower is still alive when the private hour ends, it **invades**:

- A server-wide broadcast announces it.
- It moves to the **Chaos Shrine** and **heals to full**.
- Gates to Chaos Island open at **Britain bank** and in the lair.
- From then on, anyone can fight it.

A Harrower below 5% HP when the hour ends stays in its lair instead.

## The fight

| Ability | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Tentacles** | Every 30 s it spawns a giant tentacle near a random player within 15 tiles (max 25). Tentacles have a damage aura, reflect spells (10%, 20% from creatures) and **heal the Harrower** for the melee damage they deal. They die with it. |
| **Corpse explosion** | Every 12.5 s, every fresh corpse within 15 tiles explodes for 25–35 damage to players and pets within 3 tiles. |
| **Expunge** | Every 10–20 s while it's taking damage. Every 3rd Expunge sets off the blood pools around it. |
| **Sickness** | About once a minute it "feels sick" and, 2 seconds later, fires rays in every direction. |
| **Melee** | Its hits can poison (Deadly, 10%), apply a damage-over-time (10%), spit sand and trigger corpse explosion or blast radius. |
| **Reflect** | 10% chance to reflect spells. |
| **Targeting** | Switches to a random target within 8 tiles every 10–20 s. |

It also uses the shared dungeon-boss mechanics (minion waves and group scaling, see [Special Bosses](index.md#boss-mechanics)).

## Rewards

The Harrower uses the shared [boss rewards](index.md#boss-rewards) with the **largest Void Skull table** in the game (12 special, 15 runic, 12 imbuing, 8 orb, 8 mage scroll skulls and more, including PvM rune skulls).

Its corpse also always holds:

- an **Item Blessing Deed**
- one **Harrower armor** piece (gloves, helm, arms, legs, chest or gorget)
- a **scaled runic sewing kit** or an **azurite runic hammer**
- **3 moonstones**, **3 bags of sending** and **3 high-end runic** Void Skulls
- a hued item

And sometimes:

| Chance | Drop |
|---|---|
| 15% | an epic reward: Doom pet egg, daemon statue, Wildfire robe or cloak, Inferni skin dye |
| 15% | an epic-hued item |
| 10% | a Bloodstained Shroud, a rare skin dye **and** a Zombified or Ashen creature egg |
