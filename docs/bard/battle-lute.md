# Battle Lute

A ranged bard weapon. While you wield it, it is also your instrument for Peacemaking, Discordance and Provocation —
no instrument in your backpack needed.

## How it fights

| | |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Musicianship |
| Range | 8 tiles, no ammo |
| Damage bonus | Provocation (like Tactics), Discordance (like Anatomy), Peacemaking (like Strength), plus quality. Each counts as 100 max in PvP. |
| Swing speed | Based on (Mana + Stamina + Intelligence + Dexterity) / 4 |

## As an instrument

- Bard skills use the wielded lute automatically, before any backpack instrument.
- The lute never runs out of uses.
- Its quality, wood type and slayer lower barding difficulty just like a crafted instrument.
- Unequipping it in the middle of a bard skill cancels the skill.

## Properties

Standard properties: **Swing Speed Increase**, **Damage Increase**, **Hit Life Leech**.

### Bard properties

All bard properties are **disabled in PvP**. Chance-based properties are capped at **75%**.

| Property | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Mana Leech** | Chance on hit to restore mana equal to that % of the damage dealt (max 50%). |
| **Stamina Leech** | Chance on hit to restore stamina equal to that % of the damage dealt (max 50%). |
| **Classically Trained** | Bard skills are easier: +2% success per point on Peacemaking, Provocation and Discordance. |
| **Quickstep** | Reduces the bard skill cooldown (max 50%). |
| **Encore** | Chance after a success to have no cooldown, and chance for a peace or discord to repeat once when it ends. |
| **Crank it to 11** | Stronger Discordance, and raises the discord cap (max 35% total). |
| **Influencer** | Extra damage against creatures you have barded (max 50%). |
| **Rising Star** | Less damage taken from creatures you have barded (max 50%). |
| **Amplifier** | Area bard skills: +1 range and +1 target per 10 points, stronger area discord and taunt reduction. |
| **Fandom** | Chance that damage does not break your peace or provocation. |
| **Seduce** | Chance that a successful targeted peace or discord makes the monster attack the nearest other monster (not pets, summons or legendary creatures). |
| **Carrying Tone** | Increases the range of your bard skills by that %. |
| **Extended Hold** | Peace, discord and provocation last that % longer. |
| **Perfect Pitch** | Chance on each lute hit to restore 1 use to your backpack instrument. |
| **Steadfast Grip** | Chance that a failed performance does not use up a backpack instrument use. |
| **Practiced Ear** | Faster skill gain in Musicianship, Peacemaking, Provocation and Discordance. |
| **Echo Chamber** | Chance that a single-target discord also hits nearby creatures (same type first) at half strength and duration, with some energy damage. |
| **Warlord's Beat** | Creatures you have discorded heal less (max 50%). |
| **Cursed Notes** | Creatures you have provoked take damage every 2 seconds, based on your Provocation. |
| **Bloodfeud** | Creatures you have provoked deal extra damage to their provoke target (max 100%). |
| **Siren's Call** | Chance that a monster walks away from you when your peace on it ends. |
| **Still Waters** | Chance that area spell damage does not break your peace. |
