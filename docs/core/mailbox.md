# Central Mailbox

Every bank has a **Central Mailbox**. Use it to read your letters and to write to any player, online or not. You can attach items to a letter, too.

## Opening the mailbox

- **Double-click the mailbox** at any bank. You must stay within **12 tiles** of it to read, send or collect mail.
- If you have unread letters, you're told how many when you open it.
- Walking past a mailbox with unread mail, it greets you: *"Hello <name>, you have 4 unread letters and 3 new letters!"* (at most once every 5 minutes). **New** letters are the ones that arrived since you last opened a mailbox.
- You're also reminded of unread mail when you log in, and **"You received a mail!"** appears above your head when a letter arrives while you're online.

## Inbox

The inbox lists your letters with the sender, the date, the subject and the number of attached items. Unread letters are shown brighter.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Read** (left of a letter) | Opens the letter |
| **X** (right of a letter) | Deletes the letter, after a **Yes / No** confirmation |
| **Write a Letter** | Starts a new letter |
| **Unread Only / Show All** | Shows only unread letters, or all of them |
| **Delete All Read** | Deletes every letter you've read, without asking. Letters that still have items are kept |

Your mailbox holds up to **50 letters**. When it's full, nobody can send you more until you delete some.

## Reading a letter

The letter shows who sent it, its subject, when it was sent and the message. Attached items are shown inside the mailbox picture and listed underneath.

- **Reply** starts a letter to the sender, with "Re: <subject>".
- **Take Items** puts the attached items in your **backpack**. Whatever doesn't fit goes to your **bank box**.
- **Delete** removes the letter, after a confirmation. A letter that still has items can't be deleted: take the items first.

## Writing a letter

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Send to** | The character's name. Capital letters don't matter, unless several characters share the name: then type it exactly |
| **Subject** | Up to 40 letters |
| **Message** | Up to 500 letters |

- **Attach Item** lets you attach up to **5 items** from your backpack. Use the **X** next to an item to remove it.
- Attached items **stay in your backpack** until you press **Send!**.
- **Back to Inbox** throws the letter away. If you close the window or walk away instead, the letter is kept and is there when you come back.

### Postage

| Letter | Cost |
|---|---|
| Message only | **Free** |
| With items | **1,000 gold**, taken from your bank first, then your backpack |

If you can't pay, the letter isn't sent and your items stay with you.

### What can be attached

- Items in your backpack that you could hand to another player in a trade.
- **Not** quest items, items that can't be moved, or bags and other containers. Attach items one by one instead.

!!! info "Unclaimed items go back"
    Items not taken within **14 days** go back to the sender's **bank box**, in a bag named "returned mail". The sender gets a letter from **The Postmaster**, and the recipient's letter says the items were returned. The postage isn't refunded.

    While a letter has items waiting, it shows how long is left before they're returned.
