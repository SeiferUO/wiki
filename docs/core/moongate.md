# Public Moongate

Public moongates take you anywhere important for free. Step on one or double-click it to open the travel window.

## Who can use it

You can't use a public moongate while:

- you're **criminal**,
- you're **escorting** an NPC,
- you're **casting** a spell,
- you're in **combat** and a Militia member or have any murder counts.

Turn on *Gate Warning* in your [options](compendium.md#options) to get a confirmation before entering moongates.

## Destinations

| Tab | Destinations |
|---|---|
| **Towns** | Britain, Eastern Territory (Old Minoc), Northern Territory (Moonglow), Western Territory, Southern Territory, Cove (unguarded), Delucia (safe zone) |
| **Dungeons** | Deceit, Shame, Wrong, Glacia, Destard, Sandstorm, Eventide, Exile, Hollow |
| **Shrines** | The 9 virtue shrines, **only while that shrine is corrupted** (marked **[ACTIVE]**; see [Shrine Corruption](../bosses/mini-champions.md#shrine-corruption)) |
| **Special Areas** | Tournament Area, Britain Zoo, Turret Defense Area, Bleeding Skull Island, the safe duel arenas (Britain Beach, Britain Pond, Mountain Bluff), the **Doom Champion** (only while the [Stygian Champion](../bosses/champions.md#the-stygian-champion-doom) event runs), the **New Player Dungeon** (only for players with fewer than 5 murder counts) |
| **My Locations** | Your own saved spots (below) |

You arrive **hidden**.

## My Locations

You can save up to **10 personal destinations**:

- **Unlocking a slot** costs **50,000 gold × (slots already unlocked + 1)**, taken from your bank.
- **Setting a slot:** target a **marked** recall rune that you've **renamed**. The slot takes the rune's location and name.
- Travel to your locations follows the normal recall rules: no travel into places recall can't reach, and only on the same facet.
- You can clear a slot and set it again at any time.
