# Traveling & World Map

The fastest ways around the world are **recall runes**, **runebooks**, the **Recall**, **Mark** and **Gate Travel** spells, and the free **public moongates**.

## Recall runes and Mark

A **recall rune** remembers one location. Mark it with the **Mark** spell, then use **Recall** or **Gate Travel** on it to go back.

**Mark** (6th circle, *Kal Por Ylem*):

- **No Magery needed.** Anyone with the spell, the reagents and the mana can cast it.
- Reagents: black pearl, blood moss, mandrake root.
- The rune must be in your backpack.
- Rename a marked rune to give it a name. It shows in your runebook and can be used for [My Locations](moongate.md#my-locations) on the public moongate.

## Recall

**Recall** (4th circle, *Kal Ort Por*) takes you to a marked rune, a runebook's default location, or a boat key.

- **No Magery needed.** Anyone with the spell, the reagents and the mana can cast it.
- Reagents: black pearl, blood moss, mandrake root.
- Your pets come with you.
- It doesn't take your weapon out of your hands unless you're in combat.

You can't recall when you are:

- criminal,
- in combat with **2 or more murder counts**,
- in combat as a Guild Militia member,
- overloaded,
- carrying a trade order,
- dragging an item.

## Gate Travel

**Gate Travel** (7th circle, *Vas Rel Por*) opens a pair of linked moongates: one where you stand, one at the rune's location. Anyone can walk through them, in both directions.

- **Needs Magery**, like a normal 7th circle spell.
- Reagents: black pearl, mandrake root, sulfurous ash.
- The gates open **1 second** after the cast and last **30 seconds**.
- Gates take your **Gate Travel spell hue** from your hue collection.
- You can't open a gate on top of another moongate.

You can't cast Gate Travel when you're criminal, or in combat with **any** murder counts.

## Where you can travel

!!! tip "Teleportation Runes"
    Dungeons have glowing **Teleportation Runes**. Within **5 tiles** of one, you can mark, recall and gate as usual.
    In the main dungeons (Deceit, Shame, Destard, Glacia, Sandstorm, Eventide, Exile and others), you can **only** recall out next to a Teleportation Rune.

## Runebooks

A runebook holds up to **16 marked runes**. Drop a marked rune on the book to add it.

From the runebook window you can:

- **Recall with a charge.** No reagents, mana or spell needed.
- **Recall with the spell.** You need Recall in your spellbook, plus the reagents and mana.
- **Gate with the spell.** You need Gate Travel in your spellbook and the Magery to cast it.
- **Sacred Journey.** Paladins with the spell in their book can use it on any entry.
- **Set Default.** Casting Recall or Gate Travel on the book itself uses the default entry.
- **Drop Rune** to take a rune back out, and **Rename Book**.

**Charges:**

- Drop **Recall scrolls** on the book to add charges, up to its maximum.
- Shop runebooks hold **6 charges**. Crafted runebooks hold up to **10**, depending on quality and Inscription skill.
- A **Runebook Enhancer Deed** raises the maximum to **100** and fills it.
- A **Token of Endless Journeys** gives the book **unlimited charges**. It no longer needs Recall scrolls.

**Locked down in a house:** only co-owners and the owner can open a runebook by default. Change it with the book's security level.

**Getting a runebook:**

- Buy one from the shop (`[shop`, *Character Progression*). It's **not blessed**.
- Or craft one with **Inscription** (45+): 8 blank scrolls, 1 Recall scroll and 1 Gate Travel scroll.

### Runebook Bless Deed

A runebook isn't blessed, so it drops on death unless it's insured. A **Runebook Bless Deed** blesses it permanently.

- Double-click the deed, then target a runebook **in your backpack**.
- The book can't already be blessed or insured.

You can get one two ways:

- **Shop** (*Character Progression*): 10,000 gold, 120 Reward Points or 1,250 Artificer Points.
- **Crafted by scribes:** Inscription 100, with 25 blank scrolls, 50 Arcane Dust and 1 Book of Lost Knowledge.

### Runebook Strap

The **Runebook Strap** is a runebook satchel: a **blessed, weightless** container that holds **only runebooks**. Buy it from the shop (*Storage Items*) for **10 Platinum Coins**.

## Public Moongate

**Public moongates** are free and need no spells or runes. Step on one or double-click it to choose a destination: towns, dungeons, active corrupted shrines, special areas, and up to **10 of your own saved locations**.

You can't use one while criminal, escorting, casting, or in combat as a Guild Militia member or with murder counts.

See [Public Moongate](moongate.md) for all destinations and how to set up your own locations.
