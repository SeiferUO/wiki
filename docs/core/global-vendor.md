# Global Vendor House

The **Global Vendor House** (run by **Marcus the Merchant**) is the player marketplace. You can list items for sale, buy other players' items, and post **buy orders** for things you want.

Everything is paid from and delivered to your **bank box**. You can only use it **from town** (a guarded area or safe zone).

Some vendors are **specialized** and only accept one category of items.

## Browsing and buying

Double-click the vendor (or use **View Listings** from its menu).

- Browse by **category**, search by **name** or **hue**, and sort by name, price or amount.
- Decorations can be **previewed** before buying.
- Click **Buy** and confirm. The price is taken from your bank and the item goes to your **bank box**.
- You can't buy your own items.

**Categories:** Weapons, Armor, Jewelry, Spellbooks, Decorations, Character Progression, Resources, Alchemy, Clothing, Food, Instruments, Tools, Creatures, Enhancement, Hued Items, Light Items, BODs and Miscellaneous.

## Selling

Use **Sell Item** from the vendor's menu (or **List Item** in its window), then target an item in your backpack.

| | |
|---|---|
| Price | **1,000** to **10,000,000** gold |
| Duration | **1 to 30 days** |
| Listing fee | **10% of the price + 50 gold per day**, paid from your bank when you list |
| When it sells | You get the **full price** as a check in your bank |
| Max listings | **20** at a time |

- Your listings are under **My Listings**. Removing one sends the item back to your bank.
- When a listing expires, the item is returned to your bank.
- Only marketplace items can be listed: resources, reagents, magic gear, spellbooks with properties, decorations and many others. Gold, checks, containers and plain spellbooks can't.

## Buy orders

A buy order asks other players to sell you something at your price. Use **Post Buy Order** from the vendor's menu and target an example of the item in your backpack.

| | |
|---|---|
| Quantity | 1 to 100,000 |
| Price per item | 10 to 1,000,000 gold |
| Duration | 1 to 30 days |
| Max orders | 20 at a time |

- The full cost is **taken from your bank upfront** and held until the order is filled.
- The order can require a specific **hue**, name, quality, material, minimum uses, treasure map level and so on.
- **Filling an order:** open **Buy Orders**, pick one and click **Fulfill**, then target the matching item(s) in your backpack or bank. You get the price minus a **10% fee**, and the items go to the buyer's bank.
    - You can fill part of an order, but at least **10%** of what's left.
    - [Commodity deeds](../items/commodity-deeds.md) must match the order's resource and quantity **exactly**.
    - Completed or decoded treasure maps can't be used.
- **Cancelling** your order refunds **90%** of the remaining gold.
- When an order **expires**, the remaining gold is returned to your bank in full.
