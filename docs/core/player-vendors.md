# Player Vendors

A **player vendor** sells your goods for you, day and night, from inside a house. Other players browse its backpack and buy at the prices you set; the gold waits on the vendor until you collect it.

## Placing a vendor

1. Buy a **contract of employment** from a banker, innkeeper, tavern keeper or barkeeper.
2. Stand where the vendor should go, inside a **public** house you **own**, and double-click the contract.

The vendor faces the way you were facing. You can't place one on top of another vendor or on a rental contract.

Co-owners and friends can't place vendors directly. To give them one, the owner places a [vendor rental contract](#renting-out-vendor-spots).

### How many vendors

A house holds **1 vendor plus 1 for every 100 secures**, up to **15**. [Storage upgrades](houses.md#storage) raise your secures, so they also raise this limit. Rental contracts count toward it.

## Stocking and pricing

- **Drop an item on the vendor** (or into its backpack) to stock it. You're asked for a price and an optional description: type the price first, then the description, such as `5000 Exceptional katana`.
- Press **Esc** at the prompt for **not for sale**. Only containers, books and items inside a for-sale container can be not for sale. A not-for-sale container lets you price each item inside it separately.
- The highest price is **175,000,000** gold.
- A newly priced item can't be bought for **1 minute**.
- Containers holding other containers can't be stocked.

## Buying from a vendor

- Double-click the vendor to open its backpack, then double-click an item, or say **vendor buy** and target it.
- Gold comes from your backpack first, then your bank.
- Players banned from the house can't shop there.
- Bulk order books and recipe books on a vendor can sell their deeds and recipes one at a time.

## Wages

A vendor charges a daily fee, taken once per real day from the gold it holds:

**60 gold + 3 gold for every full 1,000 gold of items it has for sale.**

| Items for sale worth | Fee per day |
|---|---|
| Nothing | 60 |
| 10,000 | 90 |
| 100,000 | 360 |
| 1,000,000 | 3,060 |

Sales go straight into the vendor's gold, so a vendor that sells pays its own way. You can also hand it gold (drop gold on it, or use **Deposit gold** in its menu). It holds up to **1,000,000** gold.

If it can't pay, the vendor **quits**. Its goods and gold go to the house's **moving crate**, or for a rented vendor, to a **vendor inventory** at the house (see below).

### Merchant's Trinket

The **Merchant's Trinket** is a necklace sold in the shop. **Put it on your vendor** (drag it onto the vendor's paperdoll) and the vendor charges **no wages** as long as your house is in good repair. Once the house is **greatly worn** or worse, the normal fee comes back.

## Managing your vendor

Double-click your own vendor for its menu: see goods, collect or deposit gold, customize, rename the vendor, name the shop, and dismiss.

Say these near your vendor:

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| *vendor browse* | Opens the vendor's backpack (anyone) |
| *vendor buy* | Buy an item (anyone) |
| *vendor collect* | Collect the gold it holds |
| *vendor status* | Opens its menu |
| *vendor dismiss* | Dismisses it |
| *vendor cycle* | Turns it to face you |

Start a phrase with the vendor's name (*Bob buy*) to talk to one vendor when several are nearby.

- **Customize** lets you change the vendor's body, hair and clothes. You can also dress it with your own clothes.
- A vendor holding goods can't be dismissed: empty it first. Its gold goes to your bank when it leaves.
- When managing a vendor from outside the house, the vendor steps out to you for a moment, then goes back after 2 minutes.

## Renting out vendor spots

House owners can rent spots to other players with a **vendor rental contract** (sold by bankers, innkeepers, tavern keepers and barkeepers).

1. In your public house, double-click the contract and target the exact spot to rent. The contract is locked down there.
2. Set the **length** (1, 2 or 3 weeks, or 1 month), the **price**, and whether you're willing to **renew**.
3. Offer it to another player by targeting them. When they accept and pay, a vendor appears for them on that spot.

The renter runs the vendor as their own and pays its wages as usual. The rent goes to the landlord, who collects it from the vendor's menu.

- At the end of the term, the rental **renews** only if both landlord and renter agreed to renew. The renewal price comes out of the gold the vendor holds.
- Otherwise the vendor leaves. Its goods and gold become a **vendor inventory** at the house. The renter reclaims it from the **house sign's menu** (*Reclaim vendor inventory*) within **7 days**, standing near the sign.
- The landlord can end the contract early from the vendor's menu.

## Finding vendors

Say **[vendorsearch** to search player vendors for the item you want.

## When a house collapses

If a house falls, its player vendors' goods and gold go to each vendor owner's **bank**. See [Houses](houses.md#when-a-house-collapses).
