# Bestiary

Every hunting ground with its creatures, its dungeon boss, its daily and weekly quests, the slayer that hits the most of its monsters and the creatures you can tame there.

!!! tip "Slayers"
    A slayer weapon or spellbook deals **+20%** against its creature type ([Weapons](weapons.md), [Spellcasting](spellcasting.md)), and a slayer instrument makes barding it easier ([Instruments](instruments.md)). The **main slayer** below is the type that makes up most of the region's spawn, with its share of the creatures.

| Creature type | Slayer |
|---|---|
| Daemon | Exorcism |
| Undead | Silver |
| Vermin | Troll Slaughter |
| Spider | Arachnid Doom |
| Dragon | Dragon Slaying |
| Humanoid | Repond |
| Elemental | Elemental Ban |
| Giant | Ogre Trashing |
