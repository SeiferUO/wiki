# Compendium

Say **[compendium** to open your Compendium. It's the hub for your character: stats, progression, quests, rankings and settings.

## Sections

| Section | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Name, title, guild and rank; gold, Arcane Dust, Achievement Points, PvP points, Artificer Points, hunger; stat and skill caps, karma, fame, kills and deaths; your active mastery and rested XP. Quick buttons: change [template](templates.md), hue collection, friend list, [wardrobe](../items/wardrobe.md), journal, add/retrieve Arcane Dust, open the toolbar, transfer Achievement Points. |
| **Player Stats** | General stats and time played, Codex points, mastery levels, event scores, monthly quest XP, your leaderboard ranks and quiver techniques. |
| **Progression Systems** | Shortcuts to Masteries, Sorcery, Achievements, PvM Properties, Mastery Ankhs, Archery Techniques, Spellsongs, Summoner Rituals, Chivalry, Necromancy and the Swords/Mace/Fencing masteries. |
| **Equipment Status** | Your gear and durability, your total PvM properties and your basic PvM bonuses. |
| **Guild Info** | Your [guild](guilds.md). |
| **Stores & Donations** | The shops. |
| **Global / Weekly / Daily Quests** | Quest progress (see [Quests](../quests/daily-weekly.md)). |
| **Leaderboards** | Top 10 boards (see below). |
| **Options** | Your toggles (see below). |
| **Server Rules** | The rules. |
| **Community Wiki** | Opens this wiki. |
| **Realm Patch Notes** | Patch notes. |
| **Journal** | Your bestiary, player kills, player deaths and exploration log, with search. |

## Options

Options are saved per account. Open them from the Compendium or with **[options**.

| Option | When on |
|---|---|
| Hunger Warning | Tells you when you're getting hungry |
| Safe Looting | Ignores blue corpses so you don't loot by accident and go criminal |
| Monster Difficulty | Shows a monster's difficulty and barding |
| Gate Warning | Asks before you enter a moongate |
| XP Messages | Shows character XP messages |
| Insurance Warning | Warns you when leaving a safe zone with uninsured items |
| Scrolls Priority | Uses scrolls before reagents when casting |
| Ignore enchanting materials | Salvaging magic items only gives Arcane Dust |
| Prevent Destruction | Auto-stores an item that's about to break |
| Skill Gain Messages | Shows skill gain chances |
| Duel Queue Messages | Shows duel queue announcements |
| PvP Event Messages | Shows PvP event announcements |
| Disable Scavenging | Turns off scavenging when you carve |
| Stealth Messages | Shows stealth overhead messages |
| Disable Transmog | Turns off transmog when you equip items |
| Auto Stun | Turns on automatic stun |

## FAQ highlights

- **Dead?** Say *I seek revival* to be led to the nearest healer, or use the Help button's stuck option to go to Britain.
- **Masteries not triggering?** Most PvM abilities use **Arcane Dust**. Check your amount in the Overview.
- **Achievement Points** come from PvM content and are spent in the Achievement Point store in the Compendium.
- **Getting murdered a lot?** Hunt somewhere quieter, or in the **Haven** dungeon, where PvP is restricted. Haven rotates every 4 hours. Insure your items with **[insurance**.
