# Food & Hunger

Eating keeps you fed, and being fed makes you regenerate faster in PvM. **Magical food** and **feast tables** give extra buffs.

## Hunger

- Hunger goes from **0 to 100**. It drops by **1 every 5 minutes** while you're online.
- At **10 or less** you get a warning (turn it off with *Hunger Warning* in the [Compendium](compendium.md)).
- Your hunger shows in the Compendium's Overview.

### What hunger does (PvM)

| | Effect |
|---|---|
| HP regeneration | +1 regen point per 10 hunger |
| Stamina regeneration | hunger adds regen points |
| Mana regeneration | up to **23% faster** at full hunger |

See [Regeneration](player.md#regeneration) for the full formulas.

### Slowing hunger

- **Gluttony** gear properties and [PvM runes](../items/ankhs-runes.md): each point is a 1% chance to skip a hunger loss.
- **Gluttony** [Ankh Necklace](../items/ankhs-runes.md): 25% chance to skip a hunger loss.
- **Dragon Meat Feast** and the **Journeyman Feast** stop hunger loss completely while they last.

## Regular food

Double-click food (or use *Eat* in its menu) to eat it.

| Label | Hunger restored | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Satisfying | +1 | bacon, cheese, fish steaks, chicken legs, gourds |
| Flavorful | +5 | bread, eggs, cooked birds, ham, ribs, pies, pizza, cookies |
| Delicious | +10 | cake |
| Delectable | +15 | |
| Exquisite | +20 | roast pig |

- Eating also restores a little **stamina** (6–8, plus more for filling food).
- At 100 hunger you're too full to eat.
- Food can be **poisoned** with the [Poisoning](../skills/poisoning.md) skill. Whoever eats it is poisoned.

## Magical food

Magical food gives a **buff** instead of filling you up.

- You can only have **one** magical food buff at a time. Eating another replaces it.
- **Taste Identification** adds **1 minute** to the buff per 10 skill (+12 minutes at 120).
- Most buffs only work in **PvM**.

| Food | Buff | Lasts |
|---|---|---|
| **Red Velvet Cake** | +25% HP regeneration | 20 min |
| **Banana Pie** | +25% stamina regeneration | 20 min |
| **Bowl of Lich Goo** | 30% faster mana regeneration | 20 min |
| **Bowl of Marinated Rocks** | −10% melee damage taken from monsters | 20 min |
| **Charcuterie Board** | −10% spell damage taken from monsters | 20 min |
| **Juicy Apple Pie** | +10% healing you give | 20 min |
| **Britannia's Finest Fruits** | +15% accuracy vs monsters | 20 min |
| **Nimble Nut Feast** | +7.5% swing speed | 20 min |
| **Mermaid Meat Feast** | 15% faster casting | 20 min |
| **Pixie Leg Feast** | Spells have a 35% chance to surge for +25% damage vs monsters | 20 min |
| **Farmers Feast** | Chance for double resources when harvesting | 20 min |
| **Dragon Meat Feast** | No hunger loss | 60 min |

**Where to get it:**

- **Cooking**: all of these can be cooked at **110+ Cooking** (Magical Food category).
- **Snack Packages** from [daily quests](../quests/daily-weekly.md), scratch cards and the Thanksgiving event.
- Rewards from weekly tasks and global quests, and Easter gift boxes.

## Feast tables

Feast tables feed a whole group. Double-click one (within 3 tiles, or in your backpack) to use a charge.

- Each table has **10 charges**.
- Each use buffs **up to 10 players within 10 tiles**: you first, then your party, your guild, then other players.
- The buff lasts **30 minutes**, +1 minute per 10 Taste Identification of the player who uses it.
- Using a table again refreshes the buff.

| Table | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Journeyman Feast** | Fills hunger to 100 and stops hunger loss |
| **Warrior Feast Table** | +10% melee damage and +10% swing speed (PvM only) |
| **Arcane Feast Table** | +10% spell damage vs monsters |

Cook them at **110+ Cooking** (Magical Food category):

| Table | Ingredients |
|---|---|
| Journeyman Feast | 10 Cooked Ribs, 3 Dragon Blood, 2 Sacks of Flour, 1 Jar of Honey |
| Warrior Feast Table | 10 Raw Ribs, 3 Daemon Claws, 2 Mento Seasoning, 1 Samuel's Secret Sauce |
| Arcane Feast Table | 15 Fish Steaks, 3 Faery Dust, 5 Tribal Berries, 1 Vial of Vitriol |
