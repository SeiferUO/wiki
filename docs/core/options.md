# Player Options

Your options are **account-wide**: changing one affects every character on your account.

Open them from the **Options** section of the [Compendium](compendium.md), or say **[options**. Click a toggle to switch it between **ON** and **OFF**.

## Gameplay

| Option | When ON |
|---|---|
| **Safe Looting** | You can't open blue corpses you don't have rights to, so you don't go criminal by accident. |
| **Scrolls Priority** | Spell scrolls in your backpack are used before reagents when you cast. |
| **Prevent Destruction** | An item that drops to 1 durability is moved to your backpack instead of breaking. When OFF, it stays equipped and can be destroyed. |
| **Ignore enchanting materials** | Salvaging magic items only gives **Arcane Dust**, no enchanting materials. |
| **Disable Scavenging** | Carving corpses doesn't trigger [Scavenging](../skills/scavenging.md). |
| **Disable Transmog** | Equipping items doesn't apply your [wardrobe](../items/wardrobe.md) transmog. |
| **Auto Stun** | Your wrestling stun readies itself automatically. You can also toggle it with **[AutoStun**. |

## Warnings

| Option | When ON |
|---|---|
| **Hunger Warning** | You're told when you're getting hungry (hunger helps your PvM regeneration, see [Food & Hunger](food.md)). |
| **Gate Warning** | You're asked to confirm before entering a moongate. |
| **Insurance Warning** | You're warned when you leave a safe zone with uninsured items. |
| **Monster Difficulty** | Monsters show their difficulty and barding level. |

## Messages

| Option | When ON |
|---|---|
| **XP Messages** | You see character progression XP messages (masteries, pets and so on). |
| **Skill Gain Messages** | You see your chance to gain skill. |
| **Duel Queue Messages** | You see dueling queue announcements. |
| **PvP Event Messages** | You see PvP event announcements. |
| **Stealth Messages** | You see stealth overhead messages. |

## Other settings

- **Global Chat:** say **[ChatToggle** to turn the chat on or off for your account. See [Global Chat](global-chat.md).
- **Combat template:** **[togglepvp** and **[togglepvpwarning** control the automatic PvP template. See [Template Manager](templates.md#combat-template-pvp).
