# Special NPCs

Custom NPCs you'll meet around the world. Most show a label like **[Bulk Market]** when you single-click them. Double-click them to use them.

## Sirius, the Bulk Market

**Sirius** sells supplies in large bundles. Double-click him to open the shop.

| Tab | Items |
|---|---|
| **Misc I** | Bottles, arrows, crossbow bolts and bandages (large and small packs), blank scrolls |
| **Misc II** | Crates of bolts of cloth |
| **Reagents** | Black pearl, bloodmoss, garlic, ginseng, mandrake root, nightshade, spider's silk, sulfurous ash, eye of newt (1,000 each) |
| **Necro. Reagents** | Bat wing, grave dust, daemon blood, nox crystal, pig iron (1,000 each) |

- Pick the items and quantities you want (up to **25** of each at once), then confirm.
- The gold comes from your **bank**, and everything you buy is placed in your **bank box**.
- Large bundles are bulk items. Double-click one to unpack it.

## Jenny, the Hair Stylist

**Jenny** sells premium hair and beard styles. Double-click her to open the salon.

- Each style costs **5 Platinum Coins** (from your backpack) and is **yours forever**: once bought, you can switch back to it any time for free.
- Use **Try** to preview a style on your character before buying. Walking more than 8 tiles away cancels the preview.
- Women see over 30 hairstyles, men about 30 hairstyles plus **beard styles**.

## Cacofonix, the Jukebox Bard

**Cacofonix** plays real UO music on request, for **500 gold** a song (from your backpack). Double-click him to see his **56 songs**.

| Choice | Who hears it | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|
| **Play For Everyone** | Every player within 20 tiles | 60 s for that bard |
| **Play Only Me** | Only you | 60 s for you |

- You can also just say **play** and the song's name near him (for example *play Britain*).
- Every song you pay for has a tiny chance for Cacofonix to send a **gift** to your bank: a bard banner, skeletal toys and instruments, Vitalstatistix's cloak or shield, a Gallic helmet, Dogmatix, his own lute and other decorations.
- To keep hearing music while the game window isn't focused, enable *Reproduce sound and music when ClassicUO is not focused* in ClassicUO's options.

## Archibald, the Global Quest Collector

**Archibald** runs the [Global Quests](../quests/global.md).

- He regularly announces the current quest, what's needed, the top contributor and how much time is left, or when the next quest starts.
- For item quests, **drop the item on him** or use **Donate Item** from his menu to hand it in.
- Double-click him for the current quest's details.

## Town Criers

**Town criers** shout the news of the realm. Every few minutes a crier calls out *"Hear ye! Hear ye!"* followed by a rumor based on what's really happening:

- recent boss kills,
- the busiest crafter and harvester of the day,
- new players who joined in the last 24 hours,
- the latest player kill, and other server stats.

Within 12 tiles you can also say **news** for a rumor, or **time** for the time of day (10 s cooldown per crier).

## Seraphine, the Mentor

**Seraphine** runs the **Companion Program**, where experienced players mentor new ones. Double-click her (or say **[companion**) to open it.

- **To become a mentor** you need at least **1 week (168 hours) of game time**, and you can't have a mentor yourself.
- Click *I am ready to become a mentor* and target a **new player** (they must still have their New Player item). You can't mentor someone on your own account or IP.
- From the Companion window you can see your apprentices, where they are (if they share their location), invite them to your party, message them or remove them.
- Apprentices can see their mentor, share or hide their location, **rate their mentor** from 1 to 5, or cancel the apprenticeship.
- **Reward:** when an apprentice earns **10,000 XP**, their mentor receives **100 Reward Points**.
- Apprentices also count as students for the [Mentoring XP](experience.md#where-the-xp-goes) share.

## Gareth the Recruiter, Looking For Group

**Gareth** opens the **Group Finder**. Double-click him, or say **[lfg** (or **[lookingforgroup**) anywhere.

- **Create Group:** pick an activity (Dungeon, Boss, PvP, Crafting, Treasure Hunting, Champion Spawn, Trials or Other), write a short description (100 characters max) and choose the group size (2–10). You become the party leader.
- **Browse Parties:** see every open listing and click **Request Join**. The leader gets a window to **accept** or **decline**. If accepted, you join the party right away (leaving any party you were in).
- **Browse Guilds:** look for a guild to join.
- Listings disappear after **2 hours**, when the leader logs out, or when the leader removes them.

!!! warning
    Party members count as allies: they can attack you, loot you and see where you are on the map. Be careful who you group with.

## Raffle Coordinator

The **Raffle Coordinator** announces the **Global Raffle**. Single-click to see the current prize, double-click (or say **[raffle**) to view entries and buy tickets.

- A raffle usually runs for **7 days**. The prize is announced to everyone when it starts.
- Tickets can be paid for in gold, and sometimes in Platinum Coins or Reward Points. **Golden Raffle Tickets** (from quests and other rewards) also count.
- When it ends, one ticket is drawn at random. The winner is announced server-wide and the prize goes to their **bank box**.

## Premium Clothing mannequins

These NPCs let you **preview premium clothing and hues** before buying them.

- **Clothing mannequin:** double-click it to dress it. Click each slot to cycle through the premium items available for that layer, remove items, or switch between male and female.
- **Hue mannequin:** use **Clone** from its menu and target someone (yourself, for example) to copy their appearance onto it.
- From either one's menu you can **set a hue** by targeting an item or typing a hue number (0–3000), and **rotate** it to see it from every side.
