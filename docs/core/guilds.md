# Guilds

## Creating and joining

- **Create a guild** from the guild menu with a unique name and abbreviation. Registration costs **25,000 gold**.
- **Join a guild:** if you have no guild, say **[FindGuild** to search guilds and **apply** with a short message (10+ characters). Leaders see applications with **[GuildApps** and approve or deny them.
- **Guild chat:** `[G <message>`.

### Ranks

| Rank | Can |
|---|---|
| Leader | everything |
| Warlord | invite and remove members, war status, buy buffs, summon dungeon bosses |
| Emissary | invite, remove, promote/demote members, set guild titles |
| Member | — |

## Guild currencies

| Currency | Earned from | Spent on |
|---|---|---|
| **Prestige** | Boss kills, guild quests, VvV battles, dungeon idols | Permanent guild buffs |
| **Prestige Store Points** | Boss kills and guild quests (same amount as the Prestige they give) | The Prestige Store |
| **Treasury gold** | Members' donations: **[GiveGold** (or **[GS**) | Permanent guild buffs |
| **Treasury resources** | Members' donations: **[GiveResources** (or **[GR**) | Permanent guild buffs |
| **Dungeon points** | Kills in each dungeon (one total per dungeon) | Summoning that dungeon's boss |

### Prestige from kills

When a guild member is the **top damager** on a monster (pets count for their owner):

| Monster | Prestige |
|---|---|
| Dungeon boss or stronger | 3 |
| Mini-boss, hard expedition | 2 |
| Mythic creatures, community dungeon monsters | 1 |
| Pit Trial | 0 |

### Resource values

Donated resources count by tier:

| Value per unit | Ore / ingots | Leather / hides | Logs / boards |
|---|---|---|---|
| ×4 | Valorite, Azurite… | — | Frostwood, Bloodwood |
| ×3 | Agapite, Gold… | Scaled, Barbed | Heartwood, Yew |
| ×2 | Dull Copper, Copper… | Spined, Horned | Ash, Oak |
| ×1 | Iron | Regular | Regular |

The treasury window shows the top contributors of points, gold and resources.

## Guild quests

Every week the guild gets **9 quests**: 3 hunting, 3 gathering and 3 crafting. Completing one gives the guild Prestige (and store points):

| Quest | Example | Prestige |
|---|---|---|
| Monster hunt | Kill 500–1,000 of a monster (dragons, liches, daemons…) | 35 |
| Dungeon hunt | Kill 750–1,500 creatures in Deceit, Sandstorm, Destard, Shame, Wrong, Eventide or Glacia | 25 |
| Gathering | 500–3,500 of a resource (rarer resources need fewer) | 5–15 by tier |
| Crafting | Craft a number of items | varies |

## Permanent guild buffs

Warlords and leaders buy buffs from the **Prestige Buffs** window. Every member gets them. Each buff has **3 levels**.

Each purchase costs more than the last. The *n*-th level your guild buys costs **300,000 × n gold**, **50,000 × n resources** and **100 × n Prestige**. The server announces each new buff.

| Buff | Per level |
|---|---|
| Spell Enhancement | +10% chance for enhanced spells to trigger |
| Summon Ability / Summon Spell Enhancement | +10% summon ability / spell damage |
| Exceptional Crafters | +5% exceptional chance |
| Double Yields | +5% double harvest chance |
| Scavenger | +10% carved resources |
| Eldritch / Bulwark Protection | +5% PvM spell / melee protection |
| Giant Killer | +5% damage to bosses |
| Eldritch / Savage / Scaled Creatures | +5% pet spell damage / pet melee damage / pet damage absorption |

## Temporary guild buffs

**Guild Buff Tokens** drop from mini-bosses and stronger (25% chance). Use one to store it in your guild (up to 100 stored). Warlords and leaders activate them from the guild window (up to 3 active at once).

A token has a random:

- **type**: resistance, damage (melee or spell), double harvesting, exceptional chance, loot (gold or special items) or XP,
- **bonus**: 5%, 10% or 15%,
- **duration**: 15, 30 or 60 minutes,
- **area**: anywhere, or a specific dungeon/area.

## Guild dungeon bosses

Kills by guild members inside a dungeon add **dungeon points** for that dungeon: monster HP ÷ 10 per kill (max 500; ⅓ for champion minions, half in the Haven dungeon). Dungeons: Deceit, Shame, Wrong, Eventide, Destard, Glacia, Sandstorm. Winning a [Region Domination](../combat/domination.md) also adds points.

At **100,000 points**, a Warlord or leader standing in the dungeon's **boss room** can summon its boss from the guild window. Only one can be up at a time, and everyone is told who summoned it.
