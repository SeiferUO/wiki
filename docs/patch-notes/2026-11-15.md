# November 15, 2026

## Highlights

- **New skill: Ninjitsu!** Linked to a codex, it's a very custom mix of your beloved Ninjitsu and Bushido. A **Ninja NPC** in Britain teaches it. See [Ninjitsu](../combat/ninjitsu.md).
- **Garden Plots:** place your own farm! Buy the deed from the **Farmer** for **500,000 gold**. Placement works much like a house, but it doesn't count as one (one farm per account). Grow food, plants, resources and rare potted plants, and soon a special material for the upcoming "Shock" system. See [Garden Plot](../core/garden-plot.md).
- **The True Britannian's Prosperity Fountain:** a new raffle in Britain. Collect coins from monsters, quests, maps and other content, then double-click a coin and target the Fountain to add gold and entries to the jackpot. Double-click the Fountain to see the current jackpot. The raffle is drawn **every Saturday**.
- **The Quest Journal:** the Quest button on your paperdoll is completely revamped. All your ongoing quests are in one place, and you no longer need to visit NPCs to start quests, hand in items or collect rewards. Some class quests still use their NPCs: Animal Trainer, Mage, Fishing and Helga's quests.
- **Login progression window:** a new window at login shows your progression: what's going on, what you've been up to and how well you're doing.
- **Armor no longer lowers your Dexterity in PvM.** Your speed is still affected in PvP. Instead, the stamina loss when you take damage is slightly higher in heavy armor. We tested it: if you keep yourself fed and have some regeneration skills, it's barely noticeable.
- **New Player Tutorial:** new quests, tutorial XP and your first home, a tent. See below and the [New Player Tutorial](../quests/tutorial.md) page.

## New players

- **Character creation:** pick your **stats** along with your 7 skills (**150 points**, each stat from 20 to 100). **Ninjitsu** can be picked as a starting skill, with a katana, 100 bandages and 10 lesser heal potions.
- **Vendors teach up to 50.0 skill for free**, so new players can try new skills and templates without paying for it.
- **The Blacksmith and Weaponsmith repair your worn items** when they're damaged. It's meant for new players, so it's only available a few times per account.
- **New Player Donation Chest** in Britain: veteran players can drop items for new adventurers. It's not a simple wooden chest, it has several functions. :)
- **Toolmaker:** a new NPC who only sells tools, so it's less confusing for new players. Tools now show the skills they're used for, and one set of tools covers everything.
- **[wiki** and the **Codex of Knowledge** now open this wiki in your browser.

### Tutorial: your first home

- Every tutorial quest now gives **tutorial XP**, shown as **+X XP** next to its name. Monster hunts give more than quick tasks.
- A progress bar at the bottom of the Adventurer's Quest Log shows how close you are to a **special reward**.
- At **150 XP** you receive a **blessed tent deed** (Blue or Green) in your **bank box**, with a window explaining your new home.
- Tents can be placed by **Young** players and before 40 hours of play time. They still count as your account's house.

## Quests

- **Mysterious specimens (tamers):** mysterious creatures spawn in random regions. It's your duty to discover the **15 locations**! Bring the creatures back to be rewarded with a **mysterious egg**. See [Mysterious Creatures](../quests/mysterious-creatures.md).
- **Unbound Grimoire (mages):** buy an unbound grimoire and fill it by dealing damage with your spells and summons. Once it's full, upgrade one spell of your choice by **1% arcane power**. See [Arcane Grimoire](../quests/arcane-grimoire.md).
- **2 new daily quests**, and daily quest items drop a little more often.

## Items

- **Ethereal Collection:** all your unlocked ethereals on a single statuette. Unlock them by single-clicking it, with platinum or with your existing ethereals (unlimited charges). See [Ethereal Collection](../items/ethereal-collection.md).
- **Dungeon Moonstones:** like the Harrower moonstones, but each one only works in the dungeon it's linked to. Channel it inside the dungeon to open a gate at the entrance for 2 minutes. It can't be used inside a boss or champion area.
- **Recall runes stack** now (unmarked ones).
- **Skill jewels** added.
- **Bottle of Gender Change** in the store: change the gender of your creatures. Yeh. ;/
- A bunch of **new hues** in the wardrobe shop.
- The **instrument storage** now accepts exceptional instruments.
- **Item bless deeds** can't be used during PvP anymore.
- **Fixed:** Paragon sets sometimes gave more bonus than intended when mixing greater and lesser parts.

## Houses

- **Blue and Green Tents** now have secure storage and lockdowns, and their sign is on the right side. Tents are worth **15,000 gold**; demolishing one refunds 80% to your bank box.
- **House deeds can't be sold to vendors** anymore, including the Real Estate Broker.
- **Bank vault:** a new **Deposit** option in its single-click menu.
- **House animal trainer:** change their gender and name from the single-click menu.

## Combat & magic

- **Auto-cast for Chivalry and Necromancy buffs:** their spellbooks now offer an option to cast them automatically, and it works with spell channelling.
- **Taste Identification** now cancels **at most half** of heavy armor's mana regeneration penalty (at 120 skill), instead of all of it. Heavy armor users can still regenerate mana, but at no more than about half the normal rate.

### Grimoire combos

Upgraded attack spells now set each other up. See the [Grimoire](../magic/grimoire.md#combos) page for details.

| Combo | Spell | In short |
|---|---|---|
| **Arcane Mark** | Magic Arrow | Marks a monster (up to 5). Your next other damaging spell on it uses the marks for bonus damage. |
| **Brittle** | Paralyze | Your next damaging spell on a paralyzed monster deals bonus damage and shatters the freeze. |
| **Execute** | Harm | Below 33% health, Harm deals a big bonus with no cooldown, and a kill refunds mana. |
| **Overload** | Energy Bolt | Bonus damage against frozen, paralyzed or rooted monsters. |
| **Static** | Lightning | Each Lightning on the same monster adds a stack for more damage; at tier 3 it arcs to nearby monsters. |
| **Detonate** | Explosion | On a burning monster the fire bursts, hits monsters around it and spreads. |

- **Explosion**'s splash is smaller: fewer monsters, a smaller area, and part of the spell's damage instead of all of it.
- **Lightning**'s flat bonus damage is lower; Static stacks make up for it in long fights. A monster it hinders can't be hindered again until 10 seconds after the hinder ends.

### Ninjitsu

- **Ninja weapons** now drop from monsters and various content.
- Perfection gained while moving (running or stealthing) scales with your **Ninjitsu** and **Focus** skills.

## Crafting & gathering

- **Crafting output container:** pick a container in the crafting menu, and your crafted items go straight into it (it must be secure).
- **Spined leather** yields are up. They had basically fallen out of the loot table, whoops.
- **Deceit and Wrong treasure maps:** double-click them for a direction, like "27 steps to the north".
- Artisan mastery gains now show a message.

## PvP & safe zones

- **Britain safe zone:** unchanged for **blue** players. If you're PvP-flagged (criminal, Militia, red or grey), only the **bank building** is 100% safe now. As always, you can't enter that area while flagged.

## Quality of life

- **Parties:** any member can invite other players, not only the party leader.
- **Gumps:** lots of adjustments. The Compendium is completely redone, the Wardrobe has helpers, and the Mastery window opens on your selected mastery.
- The **Template Manager** window was revamped, and **[template** with no name now opens it.
