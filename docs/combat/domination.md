# Region Domination

Guilds fight over **control points** in major regions. Holding a region gives your guild a bonus.

## Who can take part

You must be in a **guild** and flagged for the **Guild Militia**. Other players near the point don't count.

## Vulnerability

- A control point becomes **vulnerable** at a random time **8–16 hours** after its last capture.
- There's a server-wide warning **15 minutes** before, plus a Discord announcement.
- A capture window lasts **20 minutes**.
- Use the control point to see its status, owner and time left.

## Capturing

Stand within **12 tiles** of the point, alive and not hidden. Every **5 seconds** you earn capture points. How many depends on how outnumbered your guild is:

| Situation near the point | Points per tick |
|---|---|
| You're alone | 2 |
| Only your guild is there | 1 |
| Your guild is even or outnumbered | 2 |
| Slight advantage (up to 1.5 : 1) | 1 |
| Moderate advantage (up to 2 : 1) | 1, half the time |
| Heavy advantage (more than 2 : 1) | rarely 1 |

- **Dying** to an enemy loses you up to **5** points.
- **Instant capture:** reach **250** points, or **50** points while truly alone.
- Otherwise, when time runs out, the **guild with the most combined points** wins.

## Rewards

- **Victory Chest** next to the point. Only the winning guild can open it for the first 15 minutes, then anyone can. It disappears after 30 minutes. It holds:
    - 10,000 gold,
    - 1–2 sets of enhancement scroll + mage item + imbuing resource,
    - crafting resources, reagents and artifact materials,
    - a chance for special loot, a mastery item and a Compassion tincture.
- An **uncontested** capture (only one guild took part) gives a weaker chest: half the gold and half the bonus chances.
- **PvP shop coins:** everyone with at least 15% of the total points gets **1 coin**, and the winner gets **5**.

## Region bonuses

While your guild holds a region, every Guild Militia member of the guild gets that region's bonus:

| Bonus | Effect |
|---|---|
| Spell Damage / Weapon Damage | more damage |
| Creature Spell / Melee Damage | your pets deal more damage |
| Summon Spell / Melee Damage | your summons deal more damage |
| HP / Mana / Stamina Regeneration | faster regeneration |
| Armament Fortification | chance to stop gear property [degradation](../items/enchanting-crafting.md#degradation) |
| Magic Reflection | more magic reflection |
| Job Bonus, Insurance Bonus, Boss Slayer | utility bonuses |
