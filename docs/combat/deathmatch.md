# Deathmatch

A free-for-all PvP event that runs on a schedule. Everyone fights everyone; points decide the winner.

## Schedule

- Matches run automatically, with a break between them *(timings set by staff)*.
- The server announces the next match **15 minutes** and **5 minutes** before it starts (with a pop-up at 5 minutes). Each match takes place in one of these arenas: **Bilgewater Dock, Jhelom, Graveyard, Buccaneer's Den, Yew**.
- **Double-click the Arena Master** to join, before or during the match.

## Joining

- You can't join mounted (unless the staff allow mounts for that match).
- Only **one character per IP** can take part in a match.
- You enter at a random spot with full HP, mana and stamina.

## Arena rules

- **No item loss.** Your gear stays on you when you die.
- You can't heal or buff other players.
- No Recall, Gate Travel, Sacred Journey or Mark (and no ethereal mounts in no-mount matches).
- Logging out inside takes **15 minutes**, and logging out removes you from the match.

## Points

| Event | Points |
|---|---|
| Kill a player | per kill *(set by staff)* |
| Time alive | per minute survived *(set by staff)* |
| Top damage on the **deathless lich** | **+100** |
| Staying outside the arena too long after dying | lose points every minute *(set by staff)* |

- **On a kill** you get back 20 HP, 20 stamina and 50 mana.
- **When you die** you're resurrected in the waiting area at full health. Wait **30 seconds**, then rejoin from the Arena Master. Rejoin soon, or your points start to drain.
- **Deathless lich:** an arena champion appears at **3, 7 and 11 minutes** into the match. Whoever deals the most damage to it gets +100 points.

**Special mode:** in special-mode matches, each kill also grants a random 2-minute effect instead of the HP/mana refill.

## Rewards

When the match ends:

- **Everyone** gets **Event Points** = your points ÷ 10 (max **50**).
- The **top 3** get a **Champion Trophy** (1st / 2nd / 3rd place) in their bank. This needs a minimum number of participants *(set by staff)*.
- When the staff turn on special rewards, the **winner** also gets:
    - the **Deathmatch Champion Shroud** with **20+ participants**, otherwise
    - the **Deathmatch Champion Belt**.

  Both last **7 days** and give 5% Reflective. They also give 5% Surging and 5% Focus if you have more than 50 Evaluating Intelligence, otherwise 5% attack speed and 5% weapon accuracy.

The Arena Master shows the last winner and the leaderboard.
