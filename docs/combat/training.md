# Training NPCs

## Sparring partner (Training NPC)

A **sparring partner** is a training dummy you can keep in your house. It's for practicing weapons, Tactics, Anatomy, Wrestling and magic safely:

- It never moves, never fights back and takes **no damage** from players or pets. It heals itself, so it never dies.
- Hit it with any weapon or spell to train.
- Your **stats** train too, as your skills gain.
- Send your **pets** at it to train their skills and stats. Pet special abilities don't trigger on it.
- **Getting one:** place a **duelist contract** in a house you own. It counts as one of the house's **5 custom NPCs**.
- **Removing it:** the house owner uses its context menu to dismiss it. The contract goes back to their backpack.

## Damage tracker

The **Damage Tracker** sparring partner (tooltip: *DAMAGE TRACKER*) measures your damage output:

- Attack it (or send your pets) and a **damage window** opens: elapsed time and total damage dealt. Your pets' damage counts as yours.
- Buttons: **Start / Stop**, **Reset**, and **Stop at 1 minute** (on by default) for clean one-minute tests.
- It never dies, never fights back and resets its stats every minute.
- Pet relics don't gain XP on it, and some mastery effects don't apply to it.
