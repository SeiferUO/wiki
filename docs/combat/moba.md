# WOTA (MOBA)

**WOTA** is a **3 vs 3** MOBA match. Pick a hero, push three lanes and destroy the enemy **Nexus**. See [WOTA Heroes](moba-heroes.md) for every hero.

## Joining

- Type **[moba** to join the queue (**[mobastatus** shows it, **[mobaleave** leaves).
- You need **24 hours of game time**. You can't queue from a dungeon or jail.
- A match starts when 6 players are queued. **Parties are kept on the same team** when possible.

## Draft

Players pick heroes in turn. You have **30 seconds** per pick, or a hero is picked for you. Each hero can only be picked once per match.

In the match, your skills, stats and gear are replaced by your **hero's kit**. Everything is restored when the match ends. During a match you can only equip MOBA items.

## The map

- **3 lanes** (top, mid, bottom). Each team has **2 turrets per lane** and a **Nexus** (5,000 HP).
- **Turrets** have 1,500 HP, shoot every 2 seconds within 8 tiles, and hit minions hard.
    - Behind your minions, a turret hits you for 40.
    - **Without minions in front of you**, it hits for **30% of your max HP** per shot.
- Destroying both turrets in a lane makes the enemy Nexus **vulnerable** from that lane.

## Minions

A wave of **6 minions per lane** spawns every **30 seconds**. Every 6th wave brings a **cannon** minion that's tough and strong against turrets. Each team has at most 30 minions.

## Gold and XP

You start with **300 gold**.

| Event | Gold | XP |
|---|---|---|
| Mage minion / Warrior minion / Cannon | 5 / 2 / 10 | 150 |
| Nearby when a minion dies (12 tiles) | 50% of its gold | — |
| Turret damaged to 75 / 50 / 25% | 30 each (team) | — |
| Turret destroyed | 30 (team) | 1,000 |
| **Player kill** | **300** (scaled by your K/D) | 800 |
| Assist | 75 | 300 |

**Kill gold by K/D:**

| Your kills ÷ deaths | 3+ | 2+ | 1+ | 0.5+ | below 0.5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kill gold | ×1.5 | ×1.25 | ×1 | ×0.75 | ×0.5 |

### Levels

Max level **10**. Total XP needed: 1,000 / 2,500 / 4,500 / 7,000 / 10,000 / 13,500 / 17,500 / 22,000 / 27,000 / 32,500.

- **Abilities:** at levels 1–3 you choose which of **Q, W, E** to unlock. **R (ultimate)** unlocks at **level 5**.
- Ability hotkeys: **[mobaability1–4**. There's a 0.5 s global cooldown between abilities.
- Ability damage against **players** is halved.

### Death

You respawn at your Nexus after **5 s + 3 s per level**.

## Jungle

Jungle camps give your team gold and you a **buff**:

| Camp | Gold | Buff |
|---|---|---|
| Wolf Pack | 50 | **Bleed**: periodic damage to your target |
| Golem | 75 | **Resistance**: +10 armor |
| Wraith | 65 | **Cooldowns** 20% shorter |
| Sorcerer | 60 | **Mana costs** 20% lower |
| Berserker | 70 | **+20 Strength** |

The **Ancient Jungle Dragon** awakens about 10 minutes in. The team that slays it gets bonus gold for every member.

- **[mobashop**: potions (100–150), bandages (200), a teleport rope (200) and hero gear. You get a flask for free.
- **[mobaattr**: buy **attribute upgrades** in 3 tiers (100 / 250 / 500 gold):

| Attribute | Tier 1 / 2 / 3 |

## Surrendering and leaving

- **Surrendering** sells your shop items for 50% and splits your gold among your teammates.
- Leaving a match adds a **leaver penalty**: +5% × your leave count, up to 75%. It reduces the rating you get from wins.

## Ladder

Every match is recorded on the WOTA ladder:

- **Win:** +100 rating (minus your leaver penalty, minimum 1).
- **Loss:** −25 rating.
- Your wins, losses, K/D/A and per-hero records are tracked. WOTA tournaments run on the same system.

No regular XP is earned during a match.
