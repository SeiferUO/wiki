## Tanks

??? abstract "Dupre"

    *A stalwart paladin and companion of the Avatar, unbreakable in body and spirit.*

    - **Passive: Iron Faith (damage reduction when below 50% HP)**
    - **Q: Shield Bash**: Slams an enemy with his shield, dealing damage and stunning them.
    - **W: Toast of the Brave**: Raises a toast, cleansing poison, paralysis, and freeze from himself and nearby allies. Cooldown extends to 18s if any ailments were cured.
    - **E: Shield Up!**: Raises his shield, absorbing incoming damage for a short time.
    - **R: Paladin's Last Stand**: Near death, gains massive armor and deals increased damage.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Parry 80, Swords 100, Tactics 100, Resist Spells 80, Focus 60

??? abstract "Keeonean"

    *Minax's monstrous warlord who shakes the earth and charges through battle lines.*

    - **Passive: Tremor (Ground Stomp deals 10% more damage per level)**
    - **Q: Ground Stomp**: Slams the ground, dealing damage and stunning nearby enemies.
    - **W: Warlord's Roar**: Lets out a fearsome roar, reducing nearby enemies' armor.
    - **E: Seismic Clap**: Claps with immense force, sending a shockwave that knocks enemies back.
    - **R: Unstoppable Charge**: Charges forward, knocking down all enemies in his path.
    - **Skills:** Macing 100, Tactics 100, Anatomy 80, Healing 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 60

??? abstract "Lord British"

    *The benevolent king of Britannia, whose royal presence inspires and protects all who serve him.*

    - **Passive: Undying Resolve (passive HP regeneration)**
    - **Q: Royal Decree**: Commands enemies to halt, briefly stunning all nearby foes.
    - **W: Aura of the Crown**: Radiates royal energy, healing nearby allies over time.
    - **E: Summon the Guard**: Calls royal guards to shield an ally from harm.
    - **R: Divine Protection**: Grants brief invulnerability to a nearby ally.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Anatomy 80, Swords 100, Tactics 100, Resist Spells 80, Focus 60

??? abstract "Mors Gotha"

    *The Guardian's deadly marksman — her bolts knock enemies into walls and detonate on a timer.*

    - **Passive: Guardian's Aim (ranged shots deal bonus damage to slowed or stunned targets)**
    - **Q: Deadeye Push**: Toggle — next ranged shot is guaranteed to hit and knocks the target back 3 tiles. If they slam into a wall: stunned 2.5s and deal bonus damage. Expires after 8s if unused.
    - **W: Reload!**: Toggle — drains mana each second while regenerating HP and Stamina. Cancels if you move or run out of mana. 18s cooldown on deactivation.
    - **E: Exploding Arrow**: Toggle — next ranged shot embeds an explosive arrow. On hit: 3-second countdown begins on the target, then detonates for AoE fire damage in a 3-tile radius around their position.
    - **R: Precision Shot**: Lock onto an enemy up to 25 tiles away and channel for 5 seconds. Fires a powerful shot — damage scales with distance, up to 2× at max range. Interrupted if you move.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Anatomy 60, Swords 100, Tactics 100, Parry 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 60

## Bruisers

??? abstract "Barracoon the Piper"

    *The rat king whose haunting pipe spreads pestilence and intoxicates his foes.*

    - **Passive: Plague Bearer (Barracoon's attacks have a 25% chance to apply Lesser Poison)**
    - **Q: Venomous Strike**: Strikes with a venom-coated blade, applying Lethal Poison.
    - **W: Poison Eater**: Consumes poison from a target to heal himself.
    - **E: Poisonous Aura**: Radiates poison, applying Lesser Poison to all nearby enemies.
    - **R: Intoxication**: Plays his pipe, intoxicating enemies and causing erratic movement.
    - **Skills:** Fencing 100, Tactics 100, Anatomy 100, Healing 75, Resist Spells 75, Focus 60

??? abstract "Blackthorn"

    *A corrupted knight who embraced Chaos and now batters foes with tyrannical force.*

    - **Passive: Chaos Surge (abilities have a chance to deal bonus chaos damage)**
    - **Q: Chaos Blade**: Strikes with a chaos-infused blade dealing unpredictable damage.
    - **W: Crushing Blow**: Crushes an enemy player — they take damage on every step for several seconds.
    - **E: Fellowship of Chaos**: Plants a destructible Chaos Flag. Nearby allies gain bonus attack speed while it stands.
    - **R: Vision of Terror**: Fires a line of terrifying chaos energy — damages and paralyzes every enemy it passes through.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Anatomy 80, Swords 100, Tactics 100, Resist Spells 80, Focus 60

??? abstract "Corgul"

    *The Stormbringer, a dread pirate lord who commands the skies and electrocutes all who oppose him.*

    - **Passive: Stormbringer (dealing damage briefly reduces target's resistances)**
    - **Q: Grav Por**: Hurls a bolt of lightning at a target — freezes and damages them on impact.
    - **W: Chain Lightning**: Releases a chain of lightning that arcs through up to 5 nearby enemies.
    - **E: Squall Step**: Places a crackling lightning field at a ground location that shocks enemies who step on it.
    - **R: Electric Storm**: Calls down an electric storm — 25 bolts rain across the area, each target struck only once.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80

??? abstract "Horance the Liche"

    *An ancient liche who poisons the battlefield and siphons life from those around him.*

    - **Passive: Undead Resilience (immune to poison; heals 1% max HP per poison tick nearby)**
    - **Q: Poison Strike**: Delivers a poisoned blow that lingers on the target.
    - **W: Plague Field**: Creates a field of disease that damages all who stand within it.
    - **E: Drain Touch**: Drains life from an enemy, healing Horance for the amount taken.
    - **R: Death's Domain**: Raises the dead to fight for him and terrifies nearby living enemies.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80

??? abstract "Seggallion"

    *A massive armored warlord who extends his reach, shields himself from harm, and leaps across the battlefield.*

    - **Passive: Ironclad (bonus physical resistance while Iron Wall is active)**
    - **Q: Long Reach**: Extends weapon reach by +3 tiles for several seconds.
    - **W: Iron Wall**: Toggle: becomes frozen but blocks all incoming damage from the faced direction, draining mana each second.
    - **E: Bola Throw**: Throws a bola skillshot — first enemy hit is slowed for 2.5 seconds.
    - **R: Seismic Leap**: Channels for 5 seconds, then leaps to a target location and deals AoE damage on landing.
    - **Skills:** Fencing 100, Tactics 100, Anatomy 80, Healing 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 60

??? abstract "Shamino"

    *A fierce ranger-lord who calls upon beast and blade to overwhelm his foes.*

    - **Passive: Ranger's Eye (increased attack range and accuracy)**
    - **Q: Thrown Axe**: Hurls his axe at an enemy, dealing damage and slowing them.
    - **W: Beast Call**: Summons a wild beast to fight alongside Shamino.
    - **E: Enrages**: Enters a rage, boosting attack speed and damage output.
    - **R: Execution**: Deals massive damage to a low-health enemy.
    - **Skills:** Swords 100, Tactics 100, Anatomy 80, Lumberjacking 80, Resist Spells 60, Healing 80, Focus 60

??? abstract "SinVraal"

    *A daemon warlord who pulls foes to him, leaps across the battlefield, and hunts prey with a soul spear.*

    - **Passive: Infernal Hide (absorbs a portion of fire damage dealt to self)**
    - **Q: Vortex Pull**: Aims a cone in front of SinVraal — all enemies within 3 tiles in the forward arc are pulled 2 tiles toward him.
    - **W: Blood Frenzy**: Sacrifices 30% max HP to gain 30% attack speed for several seconds.
    - **E: Infernal Leap**: Leaps to a target location, dealing fire damage in a 2-tile radius on landing and healing for half the damage dealt.
    - **R: Soul Spear**: Hurls a Soul Spear up to 100 tiles. Hits the first enemy — if a player, SinVraal teleports to them. Damage scales with distance.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Anatomy 80, Swords 100, Focus 60, Tactics 100, Resist Spells 80

## Assassins

??? abstract "Avatar"

    *The Champion of Virtue, defender of Britannia and embodiment of the Eight Virtues.*

    - **Passive: Virtue Shield (nearby allies gain +5 VA)**
    - **Q: Soul Anchor**: Drops an orb at a chosen location after 1.25s. On landing: damages and steals mana from nearby enemies. Up to 6 active — oldest replaced at cap. Enemies can destroy orbs.
    - **W: Bracketed Blink**: Blinks to a target location, marking both origin and destination. Automatically returns after a few seconds — press W again before then to snap back early. Cooldown starts on return.
    - **E: Celestial Cage**: Fires a vine cage skillshot in a chosen direction. The first enemy player hit takes damage and is frozen in place for several seconds.
    - **R: Convergence**: Launches all active Soul Anchors at a target in sequence — each deals damage on impact. Requires at least 1 orb.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80

??? abstract "Erstam the Mad Mage"

    *An eccentric archmage of bizarre experiments who bends the rules of magic.*

    - **Passive: Mad Insight (casting while moving grants bonus spell damage)**
    - **Q: Warp Cast**: Fires a warped spell that ricochets between nearby enemies.
    - **W: Stitches**: Links two targets; damage dealt to one is shared with the other.
    - **E: Phase**: Phases out of reality briefly, becoming untargetable.
    - **R: Exorcism**: Banishes a target, briefly removing them from combat.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80

??? abstract "Faulinei"

    *A shadow-born demon who hunts the weak and becomes deadlier the longer she lurks unseen.*

    - **Passive: Shadow Hunger (gains 1% HP per second while unseen by enemies)**
    - **Q: Death Pounce**: Leaps at a target from stealth, dealing massive burst damage.
    - **W: Shadow Dagger**: Throws a dagger that marks a target, increasing damage they receive.
    - **E: Predator's Rest**: Enters stealth and rapidly regenerates HP while hidden.
    - **R: Dagger Storm**: Hurls a storm of daggers at all nearby enemies at once.
    - **Skills:** Fencing 100, Tactics 100, Anatomy 100, Healing 75, Focus 75, Resist Spells 75

??? abstract "Geoffrey"

    *A nimble blade-master who dashes twice, fans shurikens, strikes with precision, and blitzes multiple enemies at once.*

    - **Passive: Blade Dance (each ability use briefly increases dodge chance)**
    - **Q: Quick Dash**: Dashes in the facing direction. Re-press within 3 seconds to dash again.
    - **W: Triple Shuriken**: Fans three shurikens in a \\|/ spread toward a target location, each hitting the first enemy in its path.
    - **E: Precision Strike**: Toggle: next melee attack is guaranteed to hit and deals double critical damage.
    - **R: Strike & Return**: Jumps to and strikes the 3 closest enemies in sequence, then returns to the initial position.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Anatomy 80, Swords 100, Tactics 100, Focus 60, Resist Spells 80

??? abstract "Hook"

    *A feared pirate captain who hunts from the shadows and skewers enemies with his javelin.*

    - **Passive: Buccaneer's Mark (bonus gold on kills)**
    - **Q: Javelin Throw**: Hurls a javelin in a chosen direction up to 12 tiles. Hits the first enemy in its path — damage scales up with distance traveled.
    - **W: Shadow Regen**: Begins regenerating HP each second while no enemy player has line of sight to Hook. Cancelled instantly if spotted.
    - **E: Crippling Attack**: Cripples an enemy's movement, slowing them to a walk for several seconds.
    - **R: Empowered Javelin**: Launches a massive empowered javelin up to 100 tiles. The first enemy player hit is stunned — stun duration scales with distance traveled.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Anatomy 80, Fencing 100, Focus 80, Tactics 100, Resist Spells 80

??? abstract "Iolo"

    *A master crossbowman and bard who strings enemies along with cunning and melody.*

    - **Passive: Sharpshooter (bonus damage from range)**
    - **Q: Triple Bolt**: Fires three bolts in rapid succession at the target.
    - **W: Bard's Ballad**: Plays a melody that silences enemies, making them unable to trigger abilities.
    - **E: Stuck?**: Places a trap that will stuns enemies who step on it.
    - **R: Volley**: Unleashes a barrage of bolts striking all enemies in a wide arc.
    - **Skills:** Archery 100, Tactics 100, Focus 80, Anatomy 80, Healing 80, Resist Spells 80

??? abstract "Julia"

    *A brilliant tinker whose chaotic shots and clockwork traps keep enemies off balance.*

    - **Passive: Precision Engineering (devices deal bonus damage to slowed targets)**
    - **Q: Clumsy Shots**: Fires 4 wild shots at random enemies in range, dealing physical damage each hit.
    - **W: Clockwork Turret**: Deploys a clockwork turret that automatically attacks nearby enemies.
    - **E: Oil Flask**: Throws an oil flask that coats the ground, paralyzing anyone who steps on it.
    - **R: Turret Overdrive**: Repairs and overclocks all active turrets, boosting their attack speed and damage.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Parry 80, Anatomy 65, Archery 100, Focus 60, Tactics 100, Resist Spells 80

??? abstract "Mariah"

    *A brilliant mage of Moonglow who unleashes arcane devastation with precision.*

    - **Passive: Arcane Mastery (spell damage scales with Int)**
    - **Q: Vas Flam**: Launches a powerful fireball dealing high magic damage on impact.
    - **W: Firewall**: Creates a wall of fire that burns enemies who pass through it.
    - **E: Ethereal Rift**: Tears a rift, teleporting Mariah to the target location instantly.
    - **R: Fire Golem**: Summons a Fire Golem at target location. It erupts on arrival then ticks AoE fire damage until it burns out.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80

??? abstract "Medusa"

    *A gorgon whose petrifying gaze and deadly venom bring enemies to their knees.*

    - **Passive: Venom Blood (attackers who strike Medusa in melee are poisoned)**
    - **Q: Poison Arrow**: Fires a venom-coated arrow that poisons the target on hit.
    - **W: Stone Gaze**: Unleashes her petrifying gaze, briefly stunning an enemy.
    - **E: Poison Eater**: Consumes a poison stack on herself to restore HP.
    - **R: Serpent Summoning**: Summons serpents to attack all nearby enemies at once.
    - **Skills:** Archery 100, Tactics 100, Anatomy 80, Healing 80, Focus 60, Resist Spells 60

??? abstract "Minax"

    *The Enchantress of Evil, a dark sorceress who scorches foes with hellfire and bends doom itself.*

    - **Passive: Hellfire (Nether Bolt deals bonus fire damage on the return pass)**
    - **Q: Nether Bolt**: Hurls a fireball that travels 12 tiles and returns, dealing extra fire damage on the way back.
    - **W: Dark Pulse**: Toggle aura: pulses dark energy every 2s, damaging and draining mana from nearby enemies at a mana cost.
    - **E: Brimstone Wall**: Raises a 3-tile wall of brimstone that slows and burns any enemy who steps through it.
    - **R: Doom's Clock**: Marks all enemy players with a 5-second countdown — at zero, all marked targets take heavy damage.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80

??? abstract "Pyros"

    *A fire-born daemon who dashes through flames and detonates the battlefield.*

    - **Passive: Pyromaniac (taking fire damage within 2s of using an ability triggers HP regen)**
    - **Q: Flare Step**: Dashes through flames, leaving a trail of fire in his wake.
    - **W: Ember Shield**: Wraps himself in embers, burning anyone who strikes him in melee.
    - **E: Detonation**: Causes a fire explosion at the target location.
    - **R: Inferno Bounce**: Bounces between multiple targets, leaving trails of fire each hit.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Anatomy 80, Archery 100, Focus 60, Tactics 100, Resist Spells 80

??? abstract "Seiji"

    *A phantom blade-master accompanied by an ethereal shadow clone that mirrors his very essence.*

    - **Passive: Phantom Bond (when shadow clone is present, Seiji's abilities cost 10% less mana)**
    - **Q: Shadow Step**: Vanishes for 1.5 seconds then teleports to the target location, appearing in a burst of shadow.
    - **W: Honorable Execution**: Toggle: next melee killing blow restores 25% max HP and boosts swing speed by 20% for 5 seconds.
    - **E: Death Mark**: Marks the nearest enemy — fires burst damage after the target takes 5 steps or after 5 seconds.
    - **R: Rewind**: Rewind: instantly restores Seiji's HP, Mana, and Stamina to whatever his shadow clone currently holds.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Anatomy 80, Fencing 100, Tactics 100, Focus 75, Resist Spells 80

## Supports

??? abstract "Frigidazzi"

    *A cold-hearted ice mage who controls the battlefield with glacial precision.*

    - **Passive: Brittle Cold (enemies chilled by Frigidazzi take bonus damage from all sources)**
    - **Q: Frost Shard**: Hurls a shard of ice that slows enemies struck on contact.
    - **W: Glacial Wall**: Erects a wall of ice, blocking movement and dealing cold damage.
    - **E: Crystal Volley**: Fires a volley of ice crystals hitting multiple targets at once.
    - **R: Deep Freeze**: Encases all nearby enemies in ice, stunning them briefly.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80

??? abstract "Gwenno"

    *A talented bard and explorer whose songs uplift allies and silence foes.*

    - **Passive: Inspiring Verse (allies near Gwenno gain a small XP bonus per kill)**
    - **Q: Discordant Chord**: Aims a dissonant cone blast — damages and slows all enemies caught in the forward arc, showering them in discordant notes.
    - **W: Silence!**: Targets a single enemy player within 8 tiles, silencing them for 4+ seconds (scales with level). Cannot target minions.
    - **E: Resonant Wave**: Fires a musical wave skillshot that passes through all targets — healing and restoring mana to allies, leeching HP and mana from enemies.
    - **R: Ballad of Britannia**: A sweeping ballad that heals all allied players in the arena for a large amount.
    - **Skills:** Healing 80, Anatomy 80, Archery 100, Focus 50, Tactics 100, Resist Spells 80

??? abstract "Jaana"

    - **Q: Entangle**: Roots an enemy in place with nature magic for several seconds.
    - **W: Healing Rain**: Calls a rain of healing energy, restoring allies' HP in an area.
    - **E: Nature's Ward**: Places a protective barrier on an ally, absorbing a hit.
    - **R: Nature Fury**: Unleashes nature's fury, dealing damage in a wide area.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80

??? abstract "Katrina"

    *A humble shepherd of Magincia whose quiet faith grants unexpected power.*

    - **W: Verdant Path**: Draws a healing line from Katrina to a chosen location. All allies standing on the path are healed.
    - **E: Frailty**: Afflicts an enemy with weakness — slows their movement and reduces their damage output for several seconds.

??? abstract "Sylvani"

    - **Q: Life Sap**: Skillshot that saps HP, mana, and stamina from the first enemy hit, transferring them to Sylvani.
    - **W: Nature's Seed**: Shoots a seed in a direction. Re-press W to detonate it early, or it explodes automatically after 10 seconds.
    - **E: Entangling Strike**: Toggle: next melee attack slows the target's movement for 2.5 seconds.
    - **R: Nature's Wrath**: Pushes all nearby enemies away, then heals surrounding allies 3 times over 6 seconds.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80

??? abstract "TimeLord"

    *An ageless being who exists outside of time, bending moments to aid or confound.*

    - **Passive: Eternal Patience (cooldowns reduce 5% faster per MOBA level)**
    - **Q: Temporal Strike**: Fires a temporal bolt skillshot — paralyzes the first enemy hit for several seconds.
    - **W: Stasis**: TimeLord enters self-stasis: becomes Blessed and Frozen, untargetable and unable to act, for a short duration.
    - **E: Time Warp**: Warps time to swap locations with any target — ally or enemy.
    - **R: Temporal Rift**: Tears open a Temporal Rift: allies within 8 tiles gain attack speed, enemies within 8 tiles are forced to walk.
    - **Skills:** Magery 100, Mysticism 100, Eval Int 100, Meditation 100, Wrestling 80, Resist Spells 80, Focus 80
