# Harvesting

**Mining** and **Lumberjacking** gather the ore and wood that crafters need. Double-click your tool (pickaxe, shovel or axe), then target a mountain, cave floor or tree.

- You can't harvest during a [WOTA](../combat/moba.md) match.
- You can't mine while mounted or polymorphed.
- Your axe must be in your hands or your backpack.
- Harvesting reveals you if you're hidden.
- You wait about **1.5 seconds** between harvests. [Journeyman boots](../items/harvesting-gear.md) shorten it.

!!! tip "Pro tip: target yourself"
    Target **yourself** with your tool to harvest the closest spot around you that still has resources. Empty spots are skipped, so you don't have to aim at each rock or tree.

## Resource spots

The world is split into small resource spots. Each holds a limited amount, then runs dry until it respawns:

| | Mining | Lumberjacking |
|---|---|---|
| Spot size | 8 × 8 tiles | 4 × 3 tiles |
| Resources per spot | 10–34 ore | 20–45 logs |
| Per harvest | 1 ore | 10 logs |
| Respawn | 10–20 minutes | 20–30 minutes |

Each spot has a **vein** that decides what it gives. A colored vein gives its own ore or wood **half the time**. The other half, or when your skill is too low for it, you get iron ore or plain logs instead.

In **guarded areas** you only get **half the logs**.

**Dungeons** are better for colored ore: every swing rolls a new vein from the dungeon column, so you don't need to hunt for good spots. This doesn't apply in Delucia.

## Grandmaster mining

At **100 Mining**, three books teach you new mining. Each book needs Mining 100 and must be in your backpack:

| Book | Lets you |
|---|---|
| **Stone Mining** | Dig granite for masonry |
| **Gem Mining** | Dig gems from mountains |
| **Sand Mining** | Dig fine sand for glassblowing |

Choose what you dig from your pickaxe or shovel's **context menu**:

| Mode | Result |
|---|---|
| **Ore and Stone** | 15% chance of granite instead of ore (50% with a **Rock Hammer**) |
| **Ore and Gems** | 10% chance of a gem instead of ore |
| **Stone Only** | Granite only |

Sand comes from sand tiles, not mountains. In **Delucia**, sand mining has a **5%** chance to also find **Delucia Sand**.

## Rare finds

- **Mining 100:** each harvest has a **1%** chance of a rare gem: Blue Diamond, Dark Sapphire, Ecru Citrine, Fire Ruby, Perfect Emerald or Turquoise.
- **Lumberjacking 100:** each harvest has a **1%** chance of a **Brilliant Amber**.
- **Archeologist** gear: while harvesting, it can call up an **Ore Elemental** (mining) or a **Forest Spirit** (lumberjacking) to fight. See [Gear Properties](../items/gear-properties.md).

## Double yields

Many bonuses can **double** a harvest. Each one rolls on its own:

| Source | Chance |
|---|---|
| [Danger zones](../core/danger-zones.md) | Up to 15% |
| [Artisan mastery](../masteries/index.md#artisan-crafting-power) | 2.5% + 1% per level |
| [Farmers Feast](../core/food.md) | While it lasts |
| Harvesting [Ankh necklace](../items/ankhs-runes.md) | 5% |
| **Harvesting Luck** ([gear](../items/gear-properties.md) and [runes](../items/ankhs-runes.md)) | 1% per point |
| [Guild](../core/guilds.md) **Double Yields** buff | 5% per level |
| Your guild owns the dungeon ([Region Domination](../combat/domination.md)) | Every harvest |
| **Magical Pickaxe** | Every harvest (uses a charge) |
| Server resource events | When active |

[Journeyman vests](../items/harvesting-gear.md) add a chance for **+1** resource on top.

## Tools

- **Pickaxes and shovels** lose a use on each successful harvest.
- **Axes** lose a use less often the better you are: **100%** of the time at 0 Lumberjacking, down to **17%** at 120.
- [Journeyman gloves](../items/harvesting-gear.md) can save the use.
- **Prospector's Gloves** (mining): **5%** chance per harvest to upgrade the spot to the next ore. Each upgrade uses a charge.

Each harvest also has a small chance to [degrade](../items/enchanting-crafting.md#degradation) one property on the gear you're wearing.

## Other ways to gather

- [Resource Maps](../items/resource-maps.md) lead you to a rich node of one chosen ore or wood.
- [Journeyman gear](../items/harvesting-gear.md) levels up as you harvest and adds skill, yield, speed and prospecting.

## Anti-macro check

After every few hundred harvests, your character turns white and a window asks you to type a **code**. You get **two tries**.

- First failure: a warning.
- After that: you're sent to **jail** and can't harvest on that character for **8 hours**, plus 8 more hours each time it happens again.
