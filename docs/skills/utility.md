# Lore & Utility Skills

## Meditation

- **100+ Meditation always succeeds.** Below that:

    ```
    chance = 50% + (Meditation − missing mana) × 2%
    ```

- You can't meditate in armor that isn't meditable, or below 10% HP.
- Allowed in hand: spellbook, runebook, [Eldritch Orb](../magic/eldritch-orb.md), Summoner Lantern, Shepherd's Crook, mage staves, torches, lanterns and candles.
  Shields are off by default *(staff setting)*.
- Active meditation makes mana regen **35% faster**. Full mana regen rules are on the [Player](../core/player.md#mana) page.

## Spirit Speak

- On success you can hear ghosts for **Spirit Speak / 50 × 90 seconds** (min 15 s).
- With a **Summoner Lantern** equipped, a success also leeches souls (once every 10 s).
- Spirit Speak also powers summons, necromancy damage and pet resurrection.
  See [Spellcasting](../core/spellcasting.md) and [Creatures](../core/creatures.md#pet-resurrection).

## Inscription

- Copies books (skill check 0–50).
- Its real value is in combat. See [Spellcasting](../core/spellcasting.md):
    - **buff duration** up to +60%,
    - **scroll damage** in PvM up to +50%,
    - **magic reflect** against monsters,
    - **spell protection** (Protection spell).

## Arms Lore

- Shows a weapon's or armor's condition and damage.
- **Protects your gear:** Arms Lore gives a chance to skip durability loss on weapons, armor and shields. That's **2% per 10 points** (20% at 100, 24% at 120).
- **Keeps magic properties:** it also gives a chance to stop a magic property from fading: **Arms Lore / 4.8 %** (21% at 100, 25% at 120).

## Item Identification

- Identifies items and shows their value.
- At **100 base**, targeting a container identifies every weapon and armor inside.
- With **wands**, it gives a chance to echo the spell for big bonus damage. See [Spellcasting](../core/spellcasting.md#3-pvm-bonus-damage-player-vs-monster).

## Anatomy

- Shows a target's Strength and Dexterity. At 65+ it also shows their stamina.
- Adds weapon damage. See [Combat Formulas](../combat/formulas.md#melee-damage).

## Evaluating Intelligence

- Shows a target's Intelligence. At 76+ it also shows their mana.
- Scales spell damage (+0.45% per point above 100, counts as 100 in PvP) and buff strength.

## Taste Identification

- Detects poison on food.
- **Speeds up mana regen** and cancels the armor penalty on mana regen (fully at 120). See [Player](../core/player.md#mana).

## Forensic Evaluation

- Shows who killed and looted a corpse, who picked a lock, and whether someone is a thief.
- **Paladins:** evaluating a murdered person's corpse shows where the murderer (5+ counts) was last seen.
- **Bigger bandage heals:** adds Forensics/10 to Forensics/7.5 HP to every bandage (+12 to +16 HP at 120). Also boosts Veterinary healing kits. See [Warrior Guide](../combat/warrior.md#healing).
- **More carving resources:** feathers, meat, hides and scales from carving are multiplied by 1 + Forensics/100 (×2 at 100, ×2.2 at 120).
