# Camping

Camping lets you build **campfires** that regenerate the health, stamina and mana of everyone around them.

## Building a campfire

Double-click **Kindling** in your backpack. The campfire appears next to you.

- It's a Camping skill check (gains up to 120). On a failure the kindling isn't used up.
- You can't build more than about 6 campfires within 12 tiles of each other.
- A **camping firepit** works the same way: double-click it and target a spot on dry land. It has **10 uses**.

### Campfire quality

The campfire's quality follows the **Camping skill of the player who built it**:

| Camping | Quality |
|---|---|
| below 100 | Poor |
| 100+ | Average |
| 110+ | Good |
| 120 | Expert |

## Regeneration

Every **5 seconds**, a lit campfire restores health, stamina and mana to players **within 10 tiles**, and to their pets:

| Quality | HP | Stamina | Mana |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poor | 3–5 | 5–7 | 3–5 |
| Average | 3–6 | 5–8 | 3–6 |
| Good | 3–7 | 5–9 | 3–7 |
| Expert | 3–8 | 5–10 | 3–8 |

- Pets get **5×** these amounts.
- It doesn't work for players in PvP combat.

## How long it burns

- **30 minutes** out in the world.
- Only **2 minutes** in towns, safe zones and houses.
- The player who built it can put it out by double-clicking it.

!!! tip
    New characters who start with Camping get kindling and a bedroll.
