# Tracking

Tracking runs **continuously** once you pick what to hunt. Every 10 seconds you get a direction and an arrow toward a target.

## What you can track

| Type | Targets |
|---|---|
| Murderers | players with 5+ murder counts |
| Criminals | criminal players |
| Innocents | blue players |
| Enemies | Guild Militia players outside your guild and alliance |
| Monsters | any monster |
| Healers | healer NPCs |

## Range

```
range = Tracking skill in tiles   (min 5, max 100, halved in dungeons)
```

**Hidden targets** show up only if you turn on the *ignore hidden* option and have **80+ Detect Hidden**.
Their range is 10 tiles (5 in dungeons) + 40 × Detect Hidden / 100.

## Options

- **Track combat:** follow your current opponent.
- **Closest:** always pick the nearest target.
- **Different:** switch to a new target each time.
- **Ignore houses/towns, ignore dead, quiet mode.**

## Combat bonuses

Tracking is a strong PvM damage skill:

| | Weapon damage | Spell damage |
|---|---|---|
| Tracking skill (always on) | up to **+20%** at 120 | up to **+20%** at 120 |
| Against the monster you're tracking | up to **+35%** more at 120 | **+20%** more |

- A tracked monster stays **marked for 15 seconds** after your tracker points at it.
- **Stealth attacks** on a tracked monster deal **double** stealth bonus damage.
