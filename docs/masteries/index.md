# Masteries

Masteries are PvM specializations. You activate **one** mastery at a time. It gives you passive bonuses and an active ability that triggers in combat.
What it does depends on **what you're holding**.

!!! note
    Numbers marked *default* come from the Mastery Balance controller and can be changed by staff.

## Roles

Your role is decided by what you wield:

| Role | You're holding |
|---|---|
| **Warrior** | a weapon |
| **Tamer** | a Shepherd's Crook, or nothing |
| **Mage** | a spellbook or a wand / wizard staff |

Every mastery has a separate ability and set of passives for each role. The mastery window shows which role you're detected as.

## Activating and switching

- Open the mastery window and choose **"I wish to activate this mastery"**.
- After switching you must wait **5 minutes** before switching again.
- You can't switch in PvP.

## Arcane Dust

Masteries run on **Arcane Dust**. Abilities and many passives only work while you have dust.

- You lose **1 dust** every time you (or your pets) deal **1,000 + 100 × mastery level** damage.
- Get dust from Arcane Dust items (double-click to add it to your character) and the New Player Attendant.

### How bonuses scale

Most bonuses follow a curve from their level-1 value up to their level-30 maximum. Early levels give the most per level.

## Shared warrior bonuses

| Bonus | Max at level 30 |
|---|---|
| **Weapon damage** (PvM) | Fira, Doom: **+60%**. Earth, Fortune: **+52.5%**. Poison, Holy, Lyrical, Energy, Oblivion: **+45%**. Berserk: **+30%** |
| **Active ability cooldown** | 60 s − 0.5 s per level (min 10 s) |
| **Ability trigger chance** | grows with Tactics, weapon skills, Anatomy and Arms Lore. +2.5% with a two-handed weapon. Mastery Chance runes add more (max 30%) |
| **Ability damage** | +45 per mastery level |

Warriors also resist monster spells better: up to +7.5% resist chance.

## The masteries

### Fira, fire offense

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Large damage, ignites the enemy and surrounds it with fire fields | Fire field after 5 weapon hits. May ignite enemies when struck. Phoenix revive on death (30 min cooldown) |
| Tamer | Molten pets incinerate foes | Chance to ignite on hit. More fire spell damage |
| Mage | Large damage and ignite. If already ignited, it spreads | Phoenix revive (30 min). May ignite when hit. Stronger Fire Elementals. More fire spell damage |

### Earth, resilience

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Devastating blow that shatters armor and grants a melee and spell shield | May reflect damage. +5% damage per consecutive hit (max 15%) |
| Tamer | Pets build a stone shield from melee hits and are invulnerable until it breaks | Shatters enemy armor. More damage to immobile foes |
| Mage | Large damage and armor shatter | May reflect damage. Stronger Earth Elementals |

### Aero, speed

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Moderate damage, slows the target, enters Quicksilver | Stamina regen. +3% accuracy per level. No stamina loss from damage. Faster swings |
| Tamer | Swift pets that strike first | Faster melee and casting. Can double strike |
| Mage | Moderate damage, hinders the enemy for 3 s | May hinder attackers. Stronger Air Elementals. Faster summons. Better spell protection |

### Blood, life steal

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Bleeding damage around you, heals over time from the damage done | Better life leech. Heals from nearby enemies every 5 hits |
| Tamer | Regenerating, aggressive pets | Pets may leech life. Bleeding strikes |
| Mage | Damage, bleed and heal from the target | Heals when spells deal damage. Better heal potions. Stronger Blood Elementals |

### Doom, delayed burst

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | A curse that explodes after a short delay for heavy damage | Better magic resistance. Pure damage every 7 hits |
| Tamer | Eldritch pets | Hexes lower magic resistance. Spell damage converts into vitality. More magic reflection |
| Mage | Big damage after a few seconds | Stat regeneration. Stronger Summoned Daemons. Lower mana cost |

### Lyrical, bard power

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Strike powered by all bard skills. Extra damage to peaced monsters, or tries to peace them | +10% damage (more with level) to barded monsters. Bewitches every 10 hits. Higher bard skills |
| Tamer | Dancing pets that mesmerize | Extra damage to barded foes |
| Mage | Damage and a brief calm | Higher bard skills. Extra spell damage to calmed monsters |

Lyrical also lowers bard difficulty and cooldowns. See [Bard Skills](../core/instruments.md).

### Fortune, gold and luck

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Damage based on carried gold and luck. Curses the enemy and makes it drop extra gold | Critical hits. Extra gold from corpses |
| Tamer | Lucky pets | Critical damage. Extra gold from corpses |
| Mage | Damage based on carried gold | Mana regen from carried gold. Extra gold. Critical spells (5%+ chance for +35%). Lower reagent cost |

### Poison, venom

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Damages and poisons the target and nearby foes. May intoxicate. Scales with Poisoning | Poison spell immunity. More poison damage. Poisons every 7 hits |
| Tamer | Toxic pets | Chance to poison. Chance of poison immunity. Chance to intoxicate |
| Mage | Damage. Spreads poison if the target is already poisoned | Poison immunity. More poison spell damage. Stronger Poison Elementals |

Poisoned monsters take longer to cure themselves when you have Poison mastery.

### Shadow, stealth

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Damage and blind. Powerful stealth attacks | More damage to isolated monsters. Weakens monster Detect Hidden (up to 75%). Instant hide every 10 hits. More stealth steps |
| Tamer | Pets gain stealth | Stronger pet stealth attacks |
| Mage | Damage and full blind | More stealth steps. Stronger blinds |

### Holy, divine support

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Damage around you and a holy curse. Heals and cures you and your companions | Better bandages. Full curse immunity. Holy pentagram when injured |
| Tamer | Healing pets | More damage to undead. Pets may resist curses |
| Mage | Damage and holy curse (cursed monsters take more damage) | Curse immunity. Better healing spells. Stronger Water Elementals |

### Bulwark, tank

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Brief invulnerability to spells and melee (not special abilities) | Better parry (+0.01% per level, max 30%). +3.5% damage per aggressor (max 35%). Shield bash on parry |

| Mage | Brief invulnerability | Better parry. Better spell reflection |

### Artisan, crafting power

Warrior only. Combat bonuses scale with your total crafting skill, with a devastating strike at max crafting skills.

- Bonus damage and accuracy with **player-crafted weapons**, based on the matching crafting skill. See [Weapons](../core/weapons.md#4-pvm-bonus-damage).
- Better equipment durability and crafting success.
- More carving and harvesting yield.

### Berserk, near-death power

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Rage burst that grows the lower your HP | +1% damage per 2% HP missing (max 30%). Paralyze immunity chance. Heal on kills |
| Tamer | Pets go berserk: +15% damage and +15% attack speed | Pack shares rage. At max rage the whole pack enrages |
| Mage | Spell damage multiplied by missing HP | +1% spell damage per 3% HP missing (max 30%) |

### Energy, stun

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Charged strike that paralyzes the target (and nearby foes at higher level) | Moving charges your weapon. A full charge adds bonus damage to your next hit |
| Tamer | Pets absorb lightning as healing and energy | At full energy, pets release a paralyzing burst |
| Mage | Charged spell that paralyzes | Running builds energy. At full charge, your next spell hits harder. Stronger Energy Vortex |

### Oblivion, void

| Role | Active ability | Passives |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior | Enter the Void: hidden but still able to attack | +10% magic resistance. Absorb spell damage as energy. Void Strike at full energy |
| Tamer | Pets enter the Void | +10% magic resistance. At full energy, pets echo their next spell |
| Mage | Move freely while casting, and spells cost no mana | +10% magic resistance. Full energy empowers your next spell |
